# P7.2 — Live mosaic canvas — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P7.2-live-canvas`, from `main` at `e293e94`
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Summary

- **`siq-stitch-live`** (ADR-0059):
  - **the canvas** is the stage's plane at `canvas_scale` (½): `CanvasGeometry`, and each frame's
    affine from its binning, the calibration, its pose and the scale, `frame_to_canvas`;
  - **pages** of 4096² words, value and coverage, made sparsely where frames fall;
  - **the paint kernel,** per destination page over the frame's footprint: the inverse map, a
    bilinear sample, an alpha feathered from the frame's edge, and a composite that lets the
    newest frame win inside its feather band;
  - **the VRAM budget,** with the least recently used page read back and kept in host RAM shrunk
    4 × 4, and grown back when painted again;
  - **the policy,** `paints`: settled, or moving under the uncertainty threshold;
  - **readback** of any rectangle, and `clear`;
  - **the CPU reference,** `paint::cpu`.
- **`siq-gpu`:** `Gpu::read_at`, reading from an offset.
- **`paint_sim`:** a 6 × 6 grid of synthetic frames painted into a viewport PNG.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_stitch_live::{Canvas, CanvasOptions, Painted, PAGE, CanvasGeometry,
  frame_to_canvas, paints, SHADERS, paint}`; `siq_gpu::Gpu::read_at`.
- **For P7.3:** the `mosaic_live` Thing keeps a `Canvas` on the GPU's thread, paints each frame the
  policy allows, binned and flat-corrected, and renders its viewport from the pages; `bounds` and
  `clear` are its own.
- **For M8:** none: stitching registers the dataset's tiles itself.

## Deviations

1. **The paint waits per page:** a submission and a wait for each page a frame covers. Batching a
   frame's pages in one submission is P7.3's, if the frame rate needs it.
2. **No CPU canvas:** the CPU reference paints one page, for the tests. Without a GPU, there is no
   live mosaic.
3. **The frame comes prepared,** binned and flat-corrected: P7.3 wires it.
