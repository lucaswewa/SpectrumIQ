# P2.1 — Acquisition engine (`siq-acquire`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `f4a5609`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P2.1-acquisition`, from `main` at `ae0616e`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **`siq-acquire`** (new, L4): `Acquisition` owns a camera, its frame pool and the fan-out. It is
  the camera's `FrameSink`.
- **Modes:**
  - live view, free-running;
  - snaps, software-triggered once the stage and focus are still;
  - sequences of snaps, with a hook after each frame.

  A change of region or binning stops the camera, reconfigures it and restarts it.
- **Numbering:** frame numbers go on across restarts, so a gap is a lost frame.
- **`PoseTimeline`:** the stage's and focus drive's readings, fed from their status watches.
  `pose_at(t)` is settled between still readings at one place, interpolated otherwise with
  speed × spacing as the uncertainty, and unknown before any reading.
- **Stamping (ADR-0031):**
  - every frame gets the context and the pose in force at its mid-exposure;
  - captured frames, snaps and sequence frames wait for a reading after them, up to 500 ms;
  - live frames go at once, and publication stays in order.
- **`capture_next(n, save)`** hands over the next frames, marked for the recorder.
- **`stats()`:** the rate, frames lost by slot and by device, frames waiting, and each consumer's
  fan-out statistics.

## Interfaces

- **API:**
  - `siq_acquire::{Acquisition, AcquisitionContext, AcquisitionStats, Mode, POSE_WAIT}`;
  - `siq_acquire::{PoseTimeline, PoseAt}`.
- **For P2.2:** `acquisition.subscribe(name, Policy::LatestOnly, filter)` for each preview.
  Frames come in order, stamped.
- **For P2.3:**
  - `Acquisition::new(Arc<dyn Camera>, &PoolConfig { slots: memory.pool_slots, slot_bytes: full
    frame, .. }, clock)`;
  - `follow_stage(stage.status())` and `follow_focus(focus.status())`;
  - the camera Thing's `start_live`, `stop_live` and `snap` map onto `start_live`, `stop` and
    `snap`;
  - its properties map onto `configure` and `stats`.
- **For P2.4:** `select_channel` calls `set_context` once the devices have settled.
- **For the recorder (M3):** a `Policy::Lossless` subscriber filtered on `flags.save`.

## Deviations

1. **Frames that need their pose wait for it,** which the brief didn't say. A snap's pose can only
   be settled once a reading comes after its exposure, which can take a polling period. Live
   frames don't wait, so previews keep their latency (ADR-0031).
2. **`Sequence { n }` is `sequence(n, save, hook)`:** `n` snaps, with the hook called with each
   frame before the next. Free-running bursts come from `capture_next` in live view.
3. **`capture_next` returns the frames,** on a channel, as well as marking them.
4. **The context is stamped as it was at mid-exposure,** from the last 8 changes, rather than when
   the frame arrives.
5. **`follow_stage` and `follow_focus`,** beyond `Acquisition::new(camera, pool_cfg, clock)`, feed
   the timeline from the drivers' watches.
6. **Statistics are a snapshot.** The pool's and engine's registry metrics come with P2.4's
   system routes.

  duplex to the emulator. A task keeps a `ManualClock` at ten times real time.
- **The tests themselves had flaws, now fixed:**
  - two hung in some runs, because they read frames without timeouts, or let held frames fill the
    pool before a capture began. They now read in a background task, with timeouts;
  - one asserted that a pose carried on past the end of a move stayed within the move. It now
    asserts that it is within its stated uncertainty of the path.
- **The `acquire` binary passed 15 runs in a row,** after the last test was added.

That is 10 new tests, all passing, in a workspace total of 192. `cargo xtask ci` passes.

**`acquire_live`,** smoke mode (1024 × 768), in real time:
- 8.5 to 9.0 frames/s, with no frames lost;
- a still frame after a move is `Settled` at the commanded position;
- a frame during a move is `Interpolated`, with an uncertainty up to 2.8 mm;
- the closing snap is `Settled` at (−3000.0, −3000.0) µm, with the true stage at (−3000.4,
  −3003.1).
