# P7.1 — GPU foundation — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P7.1-gpu-foundation`, from `main` at `c92662b`
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Summary

- **`siq-gpu`** (ADR-0058), on wgpu 30 with DX12, Vulkan and WGSL:
  - **the adapter:** a discrete GPU first, NVIDIA's before another vendor's; the device made with
    the adapter's own limits, reported (`adapters`, `AdapterReport`, `LimitsReport`); timestamp
    queries when there are any; WARP with `software`;
  - **one thread owns the device and queue:** `GpuHandle::run` sends it jobs, closures over a
    `Gpu`, each in validation and out-of-memory scopes, its panic caught; a lost device recorded,
    and every job after failing with it;
  - **on the thread:** u16 samples two to a 32-bit word, or an `R16Uint` texture; readback buffers
    kept for reuse; pipelines made once each; passes timed; dispatches across and then down;
  - **the CPU fallback:** `Compute::{Gpu, Cpu}`, with the reason;
  - **the first kernel:** `lut`, the display LUT from u16 to u8, with its CPU reference.
- **`gpu_info`:** every adapter with its limits, the one opened, and a 100 MP frame through the
  LUT on it and on the CPU.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_gpu::{GpuHandle, Gpu, Shader, GpuOptions, GpuError, Compute, adapters,
  AdapterReport, LimitsReport, lut, wgpu}`.
- **For P7.2:** the canvas's pages are storage buffers on the GPU's thread; its paint kernel is a
  `Shader`, run in a job, checked by `fits_binding`, dispatched with `groups`.
- **For P7.3:** the viewport maps its samples with the LUT kernel, or its shader, on the GPU.
- **For P11.2 and P12.3:** `Compute`, so that each kernel has its CPU path.

## Deviations

1. **No upload ring of our own:** uploads go through the queue's `write_buffer` and
   `write_texture`, whose staging wgpu manages. Readback has its pool of buffers.
2. **"P4.3's GPU lane moves here":** no job uses the GPU yet. The scheduler keeps admitting one GPU
   job at a time by its VRAM budget; a GPU job's work goes to the `GpuHandle`, whose thread runs it.
3. **A lost device isn't remade:** every buffer and pipeline would be the old device's. The handle
   fails plainly, and its owner opens another.
4. **The CPU fallback is `Compute`,** chosen by `GpuOptions::cpu` or when no adapter is found, with
   the reason; `software` is the software adapter, for machines without a GPU.

## Measurements

- **The adapters here** (`gpu_info`): the RTX 4060 on Vulkan and on DX12, the 780M on both, and
  WARP. All bind 2 GB of storage; the RTX 4060 on Vulkan takes textures of 32 768 texels, the rest
  16 384.
- **A 100 MP frame through the LUT** (release): the pass 1.23 ms on the RTX 4060; the job, with its
  200 MB upload and its readback, 136 ms after the first (186 ms); one CPU thread 33 ms. 67
  million samples in the test, 2.6 ms. On WARP, 1 MP in 2–4 ms of pass, 256 ms of job.
