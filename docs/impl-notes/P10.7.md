# P10.7 — Colour II: luminance for registration, focus and stitching — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P10.7-colour-processing`, from `main` at `1f87fbd`
- **Date:** 2026-10-29

## Summary

What measures a colour frame now takes its luminance, and the stitched mosaic is in colour
(ADR-0071, amended).

- **`siq-imgproc` `luminance`:** each pixel the 3 × 3 binomial mean about it, mirrored at the
  edges. Wherever it is centred, it weighs red, green and blue 1 : 2 : 1: no pattern, a pixel's
  detail, and the frame's own geometry.
- **On luminance:**
  - autofocus's sharpness;
  - the stage–camera calibration's frames;
  - the flat, captured and estimated from a dataset: one flat, for every colour.
- **The live mosaic** bins a colour frame by an even factor, rounded up, so that a pixel is whole
  cells' mean.
- **The stitch:**
  - a tile keeps its frame's filter array (`Tile::cfa`), and its pairs register on luminance;
  - each colour channel composites as three planes (`Plane::colour`), each block demosaicing what
    it samples of a tile, and a margin;
  - the stitch job's channels are `<label> red`, `green` and `blue`, each in its colour's own
    window, which white balances the viewer's composite.
- **`siq-datagen tilescan --colour`:** a colour sensor's scan, with its ground truth.
- **`bench_stitch_500mp -- --colour`:** the 500 MP benchmark in colour.

## Interfaces

- **`siq-imgproc`:** `luminance(mosaic, out)`.
- **`siq-stitch`:**
  - `Tile::cfa`, and `Plane::colour`, 0 red, 1 green, 2 blue;
  - `composite::bytes` counts a block's demosaiced part;
  - it depends on `siq-imgproc` now, L3 on L1.
- **`siq-jobs`:**
  - `CompositeJob` makes three planes for each channel of colour frames, and expects three
    `ChannelInfo`s for each;
  - `StitchJob` gives them.
- **`siq-datagen`:** `TileScanArgs::colour`; `Camera::meta` gives the sensor's filter array.
- **For P10.3 and P10.4:** nothing changes. The routines take colour frames as they take mono
  ones.

## Deviations

1. **`config/sim.toml` stays mono.** P10.6's notes left switching it to P10.7. Switched, its
   fluorescence channels would lose their tints, and every earlier milestone's manual test its
   look. Colour is tested by a colour variant of each routine's test instead:
   - autofocus;
   - the calibration;
   - the flat;
   - the live mosaic's preparation;
   - the pairs, the composite and the stitch job.

   `cfa = "gbrg"`, commented in, turns it on.
2. **The flat is one for every colour,** taken on luminance. A lens's vignetting differs a little
   by colour; a flat per colour would need three fits, and the previews three gains.
3. **The colour composite demosaics a tile's region once for each colour.** It costs five times
   mono's composite, within the benchmark's target. Recorded as a follow-up.

## Tests

- **`siq-imgproc` `bayer`:** `luminance_takes_the_pattern_away_and_keeps_linear_detail`, which
  checks:
  - one colour flat to its edges, on every pattern;
  - linear colours within 1 of `(R + 2G + B) / 4` inside a 700 × 400 image;
  - a wrong size refused.
- **`siq-workflow` `autofocus`:** `a_colour_camera_autofocuses_on_luminance`, at two places,
  within a quarter of the depth of field (REQ-WF-002).
- **`siq-server`:**
  - `calibration`: `a_colour_camera_s_calibration_recovers_its_mount_from_luminance`, to 0.1 % and
    0.05°, as the mono test is (REQ-CAL-001);
  - `shading`: `a_colour_camera_s_flat_is_its_luminance_s`, within 2 % of the vignetting
    (REQ-CAL-002).
- **`siq-stitch-live` `view`:** `a_colour_frame_is_binned_by_whole_cells`, which checks:
  - half scale bins by 2, or 2 more on a camera binned 2 × 2;
  - a third is rounded up to 4;
  - each pixel is the luminance (REQ-STI-001).
- **`siq-stitch`** (REQ-STI-002):
  - `pairs`: the ground-truth scan, mono and colour, within 0.2 px RMS;
  - `composite`: `a_colour_scan_composites_in_red_green_and_blue`, against the ideal mosaic.
- **`siq-jobs` `kinds`:** `a_colour_scan_is_stitched_in_red_green_and_blue`, which checks:
  - the three channels, their labels and colours;
  - their windows, blue below red below green;
  - no filter array on the mosaic.

## Follow-ups

- **One demosaic for a block's three colours:** it would save about a quarter of a colour
  composite's time.
- **A flat per colour,** if the owner's objectives show colour in their vignetting.
- **The white balance kept with a dataset** (P10.6's follow-up), for fluorescence in colour: the
  stitched channels' windows are each colour's own.

## How to try it

1. `cargo run --release -p siq-jobs --example bench_stitch_500mp -- --colour`: 535 MP stitched
   in colour within the targets, the tiles within 0.016 px of the truth.
2. `cargo run --release -p siq-datagen -- tilescan --colour --columns 3 --rows 3`, then stitch it
   from the Datasets page: the mosaic in colour, three channels, red, green and blue.
3. The service in colour (`cfa = "gbrg"` in a copy of `config/sim.toml`):
   - calibrate the stage and camera, and take a dark and a flat, as in M5;
   - autofocus, and run a tile scan with auto-stitch on;
   - the live mosaic paints grey, the luminance;
   - the stitched mosaic is in colour.
4. On the instrument, the same with the Adimec, once the real stage is in (P10.3).
