# P1.7 — Simulated camera device (`siq-sim::camera`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P1.7-sim-camera`, from `main` at `c864c39`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **`Clock::wait_until(deadline, timeout)`,** required, and `Clock::sleep_until(deadline)`,
  provided, in `siq-types`.
  - `SystemClock` sleeps the thread.
  - `ManualClock` blocks on a condition variable that `advance` and `set` notify.
  - ADR-0018 is amended.
- **`TrueState`** (`siq-sim::state`):
  - the true pose behind a sequence lock of atomics: lock-free reads, writers taking turns;
  - the light sources' levels, with `light()`;
  - the filter, the objective and the modality, as atomics.
- **`SimCamera`** (`siq-sim::camera`), a `siq_devices::Camera`:
  - a render thread, timed by the shared clock in 20 ms steps;
  - exposures every `max(exposure, readout)`, with the capability model's readout;
  - free-run, and software or external trigger, after a 0.1 ms latency;
  - lost frames counted, and their numbers skipped;
  - settings read per frame;
  - the schedule slips, rather than losing frames, when a render runs late;
  - `stop()` resolves through a waker when the thread ends.
- **The objective in place** sets the calibration and the optics' NA and index. **The light**
  scales the flux, through `Renderer::set_flux`, which keeps the cached signal.
- **`SimCameraSetup::from_config`** reads `[camera]` of kind `sim`, `[[objectives]]` and `[sim]`,
  and applies the `fast` profile. ROI steps divide the sensor, so its full frame is a valid ROI.

## Interfaces

- **API:**
  - `siq_sim::state::TrueState`;
  - `siq_sim::camera::{SimCamera, SimCameraSetup, Lens}`;
  - `siq_types::Clock::{wait_until, sleep_until}`;
  - `siq_sim::optics::Renderer::{optics, set_flux}`.
- **P1.8 GRBL emulator:** `TrueState::set_stage`, with the injected errors applied before.
- **P1.10:** the dialect emulator calls `TrueState::set_focus`; the peripheral simulators call
  `set_level`, `set_filter`, `set_objective` and `set_modality`.
- **P2.1:** the acquisition engine is the camera's `FrameSink`. It stamps the pose from its
  timeline: the camera leaves `pose` empty, as a real camera would.
- **P2.3:** builds the camera with `SimCameraSetup::from_config` and
  `WorldInfo::from_config(...).build()`.

## Deviations

1. **`Clock::wait_until(deadline, timeout)` was added beside `sleep_until`.**
   - A camera stopping during a long exposure must not wait for it.
   - Nor may a test's manual clock that nobody moves hang a thread.
2. **A late render slips the schedule,** rather than dropping frames. Lost frames then mean a want
   of slots alone (REQ-ACQ-005). ADR-0026 discussed both.
3. **External trigger is simulated through `trigger()`,** as there is no trigger line.
4. **The camera's time zero is taken in `start()`,** and lateness is judged before the frame is
   handed to the sink. Both were found by the tests:
   - a thread that read the clock itself started late;
   - a slow consumer made the schedule slip.
5. **ROI steps adapt to the sensor:** the `fast` profile's 4371 rows aren't a multiple of 2.

That is 8 new tests, all passing, in a workspace total of 139. The camera binary passed 20 runs in
a row, in about 0.7 s each. `cargo xtask ci` passes.

**One race the repeated runs found.** The rate test first hung or failed in about half the runs.

- *The cause:* the camera judged lateness after handing a frame to the sink. The test advanced the
  manual clock as soon as the frame arrived, so the schedule slipped.
- *The fix:* the camera now judges lateness before the hand-over (deviation 4).
