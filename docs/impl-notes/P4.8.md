# P4.8 — Dataset browser and 2D viewer UI — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P4.8-viewer`, from `main` at `1565ece`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **The Datasets page** (`web/src/datasets/`), from `microscope`'s gallery:
  - the newest datasets as cards: thumbnail, name, date, kind, raw frames and images;
  - filtered by kind and name, 18 to a page;
  - read on opening, on Refresh, and on each `created` event;
  - per card: View (the viewer), Details, and Delete, which asks first.

  Details shows the dataset's manifest beside the cards: its id, maker, software and host, the
  instrument's configuration hash, and the history. It also offers each image's OME-TIFF and DZI
  exports, which toast their job's id. The `datasets` store mirrors the Thing.
- **The Viewer page** (`web/src/viewer2d/`, ADR-0009 amended):
  - opens `/#/viewer?dataset=<id>&image=<name>`, or the newest dataset with an image;
  - offers a choice of image if the dataset has several.

  `Viewer2D`:
  - **`DeepZoom`,** from `microscope`'s:
    - OpenSeadragon, loaded on first use;
    - one tiled image per channel, composited `lighter`;
    - a new URL loaded hidden and swapped in once loaded, a stale one dropped;
    - the pointer's pixel and the zoom reported, and the viewer destroyed on unmount.
  - **The LUT store:** per channel, visibility, colour, window and gamma, from the image's own,
    applied to the tile URLs once the controls rest for 250 ms; visibility at once; Reset.
  - **`ChannelControls`:** a checkbox, a colour picker, black and white points, and a gamma slider,
    per channel.
  - **z and t sliders,** when the image has planes, applied once they rest.
  - **The scale bar,** from the pixel size and the zoom.
  - **The probe:** the true values of the visible channels under the pointer, once it rests,
    stale requests cancelled.
  - **The Layers menu and the overlay layers,** given as a prop.
- **The overlay layers** (`web/src/overlay/`), imported, with their storage key and ADR references
  made SpectrumIQ's. M8 fills them.
- **`GET /api/siq/data/{dataset}/{image}/info`:** the image's size, sample type, levels, pixel
  size, origin and channels, which the viewer starts from.
- **`/#/dev/viewer2d`:** the viewer on a dataset of the connected server, with a demo overlay layer
  of the image's 512-pixel chunks.
- **OpenSeadragon 6.1.1** (BSD-3-Clause), installed with `npm install --before=2026-10-11`.
- **The theme** gains `--color-on-overlay`, for text over images.

  LUT store.

## Deviations

1. **The folder is `src/viewer2d/`,** as architecture §11 names it, so the imported `DeepZoom.vue`
   moved from `src/viewer/`, where the import commit put it.
2. **The datasets page reads the newest 500 at once,** and filters and pages them in the browser.
   The `summary` action pages on the server, but has no filters yet. Past 500, the page says it
   shows the newest of the total.
3. **No thumbnails-only mode:** `microscope`'s gallery had one, and the brief doesn't ask for it.
4. **The image's metadata needed a route,** `…/info`. The viewer needs each channel's window and
   colour, the planes and the pixel size, which no route gave.

- **`cargo xtask ci` passes:** 357 web unit tests.
- **In Edge** (Playwright, headless), against the release service with the web build. The dataset
  is `export_ometiff`'s: 12 000 × 8000, two channels (green rings, a magenta grid every 500 pixels),
  seven levels. In both themes:
  - the datasets page showed its card with its thumbnail;
  - Details showed the manifest and the history (`pyramid`, `export_ome_tiff`);
  - View opened `/#/viewer?dataset=…&image=synthetic`;
  - OpenSeadragon asked for 66 tiles, each with its channel's mapping, such as
    `…/0/0/0_files/8/0_0.jpeg?min=0&max=16000&gamma=1&cmap=00ff00`;
  - the composite was drawn: green rings, the grid in magenta, white where they cross;
  - the probe at the centre read `(6000, 4000) · rings 16000 · grid 12000`, as the pattern gives;
  - setting the rings' black point to 8000 fetched 33 new tiles, all with `min=8000`, and the rings
    narrowed;
  - the scale bar read 500 µm, then 200 µm zoomed in;
  - no console errors.

  `/#/dev/viewer2d` under Vite drew the demo overlay's chunk outlines over the image.
- **The milestone's "viewable in deep zoom":** P4.7 measured a 100 MP snap's first tile at 1.46 s
  through the API. The viewer opens such a dataset in the same way.
