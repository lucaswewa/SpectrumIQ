## Summary

- **The end-to-end tests** (`web/e2e/`, ADR-0069):
  - Playwright, in the installed Edge, against a debug server of this checkout serving the fresh
    web build. The instrument is a fresh copy of `config/sim.toml` in the temporary folder: a
    2048 × 1536 camera rendered fast, and a 15 s token timeout.
  - A setup adds the users, calibrates so that the wizard stays shut, and snaps a dataset.
  - The scenarios: the login; live view; two browsers handing the token over; a silent holder
    losing it; and the connection budget, with the 3 streams, the sockets and the tiles at once.
  - Opt-in: `cargo xtask ci --e2e`, or `npm run test:e2e`.
- **`cargo xtask soak [--viewers 8] [--minutes 30] [--smoke]`:**
  - its own release service, with an operator scanning and looking at live view;
  - viewers following both streams, observing the camera and the stage, and panning about a
    snap's tiles;
  - the service sampled every 5 s;
  - a report against ADR-0069's targets, printed and in `target/soak/report.json`, failing if one
    is missed.
- **`/api/siq/system/memory`** gains the process's CPU time, `cpu_s`.
- **The live image** says which stream it shows, `data-stream`, for the tests.

## Deviations

1. **The latency target is on the pipeline's work, not on the latency from mid-exposure.** On
   the simulator, the latency from mid-exposure includes its rendering of each 100 MP frame on the
   CPU, about a quarter of a second, which a camera's readout doesn't take. The work's p95 ≤ 100 ms,
   with a readout at 9 fps, the LAN and decoding, adds up to REQ-NFR-004's 250 ms (ADR-0069).
2. **The operator looks at live view for 5 s between scans.** Scans back to back took the camera
   throughout, so live view barely ran: 25 frames in 20 s, all of them the scans'.
3. **The operator is an admin,** to delete each scan's dataset. 30 minutes of scans at 100 MP would
   otherwise fill the disk. Auto-stitch is off for the same reason.
4. **Each end-to-end test starts with the token free and live view stopped,** through an automatic
   fixture. A test that failed left its holder in place for the next.
5. **The soak's clients are blocking:** HTTP/1.0, so no chunked decoding is needed, and
   `tungstenite`'s client for the sockets, already in the lockfile. xtask gets no async runtime.
