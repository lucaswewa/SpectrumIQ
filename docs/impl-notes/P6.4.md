- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `1a7fb4c`)

- **Date:** 2026-10-26

## Summary

- **The engine's sequences** (ADR-0055), steps that hold steps and nest in any order:
  - **`z_stack`:** planes about where the focus is, `z` numbered from the bottom whichever way they
    go, each focus move unidirectional; then the focus back to the middle. A tile scan's
    `z_stack` takes one at each tile;
  - **`multi_position`:** at each position, the stage there, the position's focus, an optional
    autofocus, then the steps, whose places (moves, regions, focus maps) are about the position;
  - **`time_lapse`:** points `interval_s` apart from the first, `count` of them or as many as begin
    within `duration_s`. A late point runs within its own interval or is skipped (`skip`), or
    slides the schedule (`shift`). A pause holds a point past its time; a cancel stops the wait
    at once.
- **`p` numbers every position and tile of a plan in order:** two tile scans in a plan no longer
  collide.
- **`layout.json`** is a `Layout`: the grids with their first `p`, the positions, the focus maps,
  and each time-lapse's points, due and begun, and those skipped.
- **The dataset's kind:** `tile_scan` with a scan, `z_stack` with stacks alone, `sequence`
  otherwise.
- **`timelapse_sim`:** three points every 4 s at two positions, a five-plane stack at each.

- **For P6.6 and P6.7:** the setup page composes them, and the run monitor shows each time
  point's schedule from the progress and `layout.json`.

## Deviations

1. **No `about` for a stack:** "a range around the current or autofocus z" is a stack after an
   `autofocus` step, or in a tile scan whose tiles are autofocused or on a surface. The stack is
   about the focus as it is.
2. **A multi-position's places are relative to each position,** beyond the brief, so that a tile
   scan about each position is one plan.
3. **The schedule is on tokio's clock,** not the devices' `Clock`: the same in a run, and paused
   in tests.
4. **Tokio's `test-util` feature** joins `siq-workflow`'s dev-dependencies, for the paused clock.
   No new crate.
