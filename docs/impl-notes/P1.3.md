# P1.3 — Device traits (`siq-devices`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `fd66d2d`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P1.3-device-traits`, from `main` at `60139e2`
- **Date:** 2026-10-23

## Summary

- **Dyn-compatible async traits:** `Camera`, `Stage`, `FocusDrive`, `Illumination`, `Changer`
  (as `FilterWheel` and `ObjectiveTurret`) and `ModalityUnit`.
  - Every operation returns `BoxFuture<'_, DeviceResult<T>>` and takes `&self`.
  - Every device is a `Device`, with a `DeviceInfo`.
- **Capability descriptors:**
  - `CameraCapabilities`, with `check(&settings)` and a `FrameRateModel` that gives ROI and binning
    rates;
  - `Travel`;
  - changer position names;
  - modality modes, a `PatternSet` and `Modality::check`.
- **Status watches:** `status()` returns a `tokio::sync::watch::Receiver`.
  - `AxisStatus { position, state, homed, ts }` for the stage (`Xyz`) and the focus drive (µm).
  - Moves resolve once settled, with the settled status.
- **Frames:** `Camera::start(pool, Arc<dyn FrameSink>)`. The camera hands each filled slot and its
  metadata to the sink, which is P2.1's acquisition engine. `CameraSettings::frame_meta` builds
  the camera's part of the metadata.
- **`DeviceError`:** `Timeout`, `Disconnected`, `Fault`, `InvalidArgument`, `Busy`,
  `Unsupported`, `Alarm(code)` and `Stopped`.
- **`mock`:** mocks of every trait that complete at once.
  - They check their arguments, timestamp on a given clock, and fail on demand (`fail_next`).
  - `MockCamera` also has `emit` and `lose_frame`, and `small_sensor()` describes a camera for
    tests.
- **In `siq-types`:** `MicrometersPerSecond`, and `ModalityMode`, moved from `siq-config`, which
  re-exports it.
- **The workspace's tokio entry** no longer names features; each crate names its own.

## Interfaces

- **API:** `siq_devices::*`, including `watch`, and `siq_devices::mock::*`.
- **Drivers implement the traits:**
  - P1.7 `SimCamera` calls `CameraCapabilities::check`, `FrameRateModel::max_rate` and
    `CameraSettings::frame_meta`;
  - P1.9 GRBL `Stage` feeds the watch from its `?` polling;
  - P1.10 `AsciiFocusDrive` and the peripheral simulators;
  - P10.1 Euresys `Camera` installs its own release sink on the pool, and hands frames to the
    `FrameSink`.
- **P2.1's acquisition engine:**
  - is the `FrameSink`: it stamps the pose and context, publishes, and passes the frame to the
    fan-out;
  - reads `AxisStatus` from the watches into its pose timeline;
  - implements `Snap` as `trigger()` in software mode;
  - counts lost frames from `CameraStats`.
- **P2.3's server** picks drivers from the configuration and exposes the descriptors in Thing
  descriptions.

## Deviations

1. **`start(pool, ReleaseSink)` became `start(pool, Arc<dyn FrameSink>)`.** The release sink is
   the driver's own business, and the camera needs a place to hand its frames (ADR-0023).
2. **No `snap()`.** A snap is `trigger()` in software mode, through the same sink as every frame.
3. **Moves and homing return the settled status.**
4. **Added:** `DeviceError::Stopped`, `AxisStatus::homed`, `Camera::settings`, the `Changer`
   supertrait, and `MockCamera::emit` and `lose_frame`.
5. **`ModalityMode` moved to `siq-types`.** The `siq-config` schema snapshot is unchanged.
6. **The budget estimate was ~700; the phase counts 908.**
   - `mock.rs` is 465 of them: seven mocks, laid out by rustfmt's default heuristics.
   - A macro already shares the mocks' `Device` and `fail_next` code. Further macros would hide
     the mocks that engine authors read.
   - It is below the 1000-line limit.
