## Interfaces

- **`JogController`:**
  - `press(source, direction)`, `release(source)`, `releaseAll()` and `cancel()`;
  - its options are `step`, `jog`, `stop`, `steps`, `hold` and `rate`.
- **`Stepper`:** `add(by)`, `clear()` and `idle()`.
- **`withinTravel(by, at, travel)`** works on one axis; **`stageWithinTravel`** on the stage's
  three.
- **`useJog()`** returns `Motion` (`controller`, `focus`, `stop`) and provides it to
  `useMotion()`.
- **`clickMove(point, optics)`** takes the region, the pixel size, the magnification and the signs.
  `NOMINAL_MAGNIFICATION` is 10.
- **`useNavigationPreferences`:**
  - `steps`: `{ xy, z, focus }`;
  - `invert`: `{ x, y }`;
  - `signs()` and `signedSteps()`;
  - kept under `spectrumiq.navigation`.
- **`useSavedPositions`:** `positions`, `add(at, focus)` and `remove(id)`, kept under
  `spectrumiq.positions`.
- **For later:**
  - P5.4 replaces `clickMove`'s nominal mapping with the calibration;
  - P6.5 moves the saved positions to the server.

## Deviations

1. **The hold is a velocity jog,** not `microscope`'s relative jogs renewed every 300 ms:
   - a `move_by` holds the global lock until it ends, so a renewal would be refused;
   - the stage has a velocity jog.
2. **The d-pad's focus buttons are coarse-Z buttons.** The brief puts the focus on the wheel and
   coarse z on separate buttons. Focus − and + buttons next to the focus readout keep the focus
   reachable from the keyboard.
3. **Set Home is gone,** since the stage has no `set_zero`. Cancel is gone too: STOP cancels any
   move.
4. **Inversion covers x and y only,** and applies to click-to-move as well.
5. **Moves are cut to the travel on the client.** The simulator's stage sits at the top of its
   travel (x and y from −100 000 to 0), where a step outward was refused with an error toast at
   each tap.
6. **Esc doesn't appear in the shortcut help.** It isn't in the registry (ADR-0039). The button
   says so in its title, and carries `aria-keyshortcuts`.
7. **The Live page now needs the shell's shortcut registry and overlays.**
   - Its tests mount it through `tests/unit/shell.ts`.
   - The shortcut help's test lists the Stage group, since `/` shows the Live page.
