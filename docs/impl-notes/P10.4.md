# P10.4 — (Optional) Hardware trigger and strobe — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review: a driver phase is Done once the owner has run its "How to try it" on the
  instrument ([the camera runbook's step 7a](../hardware/camera-bringup.md))
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P10.4-trigger-strobe`, from `main` at `4117b0b`
- **Date:** 2026-10-29

## Summary

- **The strobe** (ADR-0072): `[camera.strobe]`, a pulse on one of the grabber's output lines in
  each triggered exposure, timed by its camera controller (`StrobeDelay`, `StrobeDuration`, from
  the exposure). The line's source is `Device0Strobe` when triggered, and `Low` free-running.
- **The trigger's latency, measured:**
  - with P10.1's 20 ms tick, a software trigger waited for the camera's thread's `pop`, and its
    exposure started up to 20.9 ms after the call;
  - the tick is now 2 ms, and the exposure starts 0.45–1.05 ms after the call, 0.60 on average.
- **Checked without wiring:** the grabber counts the camera's triggers and the strobes, and a line
  input tool reads the strobe's line back, counting its edges. Each trigger made one of each.
- **`strobe_timing`** measures it all on the camera, and puts back everything it changes.
- **Continuous scanning, designed only** (ADR-0072):
  - the camera triggered at positions, by the stage's encoders through the Coaxlink's quadrature
    decoder, or by grblHAL's synchronised output;
  - a strobe of a few microseconds against the motion's blur;
  - what it waits for: the encoders, the illumination and the controller's firmware.

## Interfaces

- **`siq-euresys`:**
  - `Strobe { line, delay, duration }`;
  - `EuresysCamera::with_strobe(strobe)` and `with_grabber(read)`;
  - the tick is 2 ms.
- **`siq-config`:** `EuresysCameraConfig::strobe`, a `StrobeConfig { line, delay_ms, duration_ms }`.
- **`siq-server`:** the Euresys camera takes its configured strobe.
- **For M11:** none.

## Deviations

1. **The strobe isn't seen on a scope,** nor does it light anything yet. The illumination is
   simulated, and its strobe input isn't wired. The grabber's own counts, and the line read back
   through a line input tool, show a pulse for each trigger on `IOUT11`.
2. **A hardware trigger from a line isn't built:** nothing is wired to the grabber's inputs. The
   continuous scanning's design says how one would be: a line input tool, or `QDC1`, as the cycle's
   trigger source.
3. **The latency is the software trigger's,** from the call to the exposure's start, by the
   grabber's stamps (P10.2). The brief's trigger-to-exposure latency of a hardware trigger is the
   camera's own, microseconds, below what the stamps resolve.
4. **The tick changed for every mode,** not only when triggered: see ADR-0072's alternatives.

## Tests

- **`siq-euresys` `tests/camera.rs`:**
  `a_strobe_follows_the_triggered_exposure_and_stays_low_free_running`, which checks:
  - triggered, the controller's delay and duration are set, and the line's source is the strobe;
  - a new exposure moves the duration;
  - free-running, the line goes low and no strobe is set.
- **`siq-euresys` `tests/hardware.rs`:** `each_trigger_strobes_its_line_and_exposes_soon_after`,
  on the camera (REQ-ACQ-006):
  - 15 triggers make 15 camera triggers, 15 strobes and 15 edges on `IOUT11`;
  - each exposure starts within 2 ms of its call;
  - the camera's settings, and the grabber's it changes, are put back.

  The five tests of P10.1 and P10.2 pass with the 2 ms tick.
- **Snapshots:** the configuration's schema, with `strobe`.

## Measurements

On the Adimec through the Coaxlink, a 2048 × 2048 region at 5 ms, triggers 200 ms apart
(`strobe_timing`):

| What | The 20 ms tick, 20 triggers | The 2 ms tick, 40 triggers |
|---|---|---|
| The trigger call | 0.07–20.7 ms, 7.0 on average | 0.06–0.93 ms, 0.46 on average |
| From the call to the exposure's start | 0.40–20.9 ms, 6.6 on average | 0.45–1.05 ms, 0.60 on average, 0.13 rms |
| Camera triggers, strobes, edges on `IOUT11` | 20, 20, 20 | 40, 40, 40 |

## How to try it

1. `cargo run --release -p siq-euresys --features sdk --example strobe_timing -- --triggers 40`:
   each exposure about 0.6 ms after its call, and 40 triggers, strobes and edges.
2. The hardware tests (`siq-euresys`'s README): six pass.
3. With the illumination's strobe input wired to `IOUT11`, `strobe = { line = "IOUT11" }` in the
   service's `[camera]`: a snap's light pulses for its exposure, and stays off in live view.
