# P4.1 — Datasets and the raw scratch writer — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `6b5c500`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P4.1-datasets`, from `main` at `09fe2d7`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **A new crate, `siq-storage`** (L1 Core), per ADR-0041:
  - **`manifest`:** `Manifest`, versioned (`spectrumiq.dataset`, version 1).
    - It records the id, kind, name, created, user and provenance; the instrument and calibration
      `Snapshot`s, each with the SHA-256 of its JSON; the plan; and the processing history
      (`ProcessingRecord`, with its `ProcessingState`).
    - `read` refuses another schema or a newer version.
    - `write` is atomic: a temporary file, flushed, renamed over the manifest.
  - **`dataset`:** `Dataset::create(data_root, NewDataset)` makes
    `datasets/<yyyymmdd-hhmmss>_<slug>_<id8>/` with `raw`, `zarr`, `exports` and `qa`, and its
    manifest. `open` and `update` (atomic) go with it.
  - **`raw`:**
    - `FrameKey` names a frame's files (`t0000_c00_z000_p00012`), and `Sidecar` is its JSON;
    - `FrameWrite`, with `DirectIo`: unbuffered, write-through, straight from the slot;
    - `DatasetWriter`: a thread consuming a lossless subscriber, the free space checked before it
      starts and before each frame, stopping cleanly on a failed write;
    - `DatasetReader`, listing the frames with a sidecar in key order, and `RawFrame::map` →
      `MappedFrame` with `image_u16` or `image_u8`.
  - **`catalog`:** `Catalog` scans a data root, and the writer `record`s into it when it
    finishes. It also has `refresh`, `list` (newest first) and `get`.
  - **`disk`:** `free_space(path)`, through `GetDiskFreeSpaceExW`.
- **`siq-buffer`:** `FrameLease::as_padded_bytes`, the image rounded up to whole pages, for
  direct I/O.
- **`siq-config`:** `[server] min_free_gb`, 20 by default, which can't be negative.

## Deviations

1. **Raw frames go in the dataset's `raw/` folder,** on the data root's disk, as architecture
   §8.1 lays out. The `scratch` setting stays for processing temporaries (ADR-0041).
2. **The default key** numbers frames in arrival order (`p`), on their channel's id (`c`), with
   `t` and `z` at 0. Until M6, nothing else knows where a frame belongs.
3. **Mapping needs one `unsafe` call,** `memmap2::Mmap::map`, and viewing the bytes as `u16`
   another. ADR-0041 argues their soundness, and ADR-0007 is amended to allow them.
4. **A raw file's padding** holds whatever the slot held after the image. The slot's memory is
   always initialized: it is zeroed when allocated.
