# P7.3 — Viewport rendering and the `mosaic_live` Thing — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P7.3-mosaic-live`, from `main` at `8b908e5`
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Summary

- **`siq-stitch-live`** (ADR-0059, amended):
  - **the viewport,** `Viewport { centre, zoom, width, height }`: a stage point in µm, view pixels
    a canvas pixel, and a size up to 1920 × 1080, checked, and placed on the canvas as a `View`;
  - **the view kernel,** `canvas.render`: a dispatch per page the view meets, over only the view's
    pixels that page may show; each view pixel the canvas pixel its centre falls on, through the
    LUT to a byte, times its coverage; four pixels a word; pages in host RAM uploaded and drawn at
    their shrunk detail;
  - **the frames' preparation,** `Preparer`: binned by the whole factor nearest to the canvas's
    scale, then `(value − dark) × gain` with the flat's gains kept for the frames' geometry;
  - **the feather** at most an eighth of the frame's shorter side.
- **`siq-server`:** the `mosaic_live` Thing:
  - `enabled`, which opens the GPU the first time and starts the painter: a latest-only consumer
    of the frames on a thread of its own, each frame prepared on the frame-path pool and painted
    on the GPU's thread;
  - `viewport`, `canvas_scale`, `max_uncertainty_px`; `bounds`, `px_per_um`, `stats`;
  - `clear`; `save_snapshot`, an OME-Zarr image of a new `mosaic` dataset, and its pyramid;
  - the MJPEG `view`, rendered in the display's window at most 10 times a second when something
    changed;
  - `Instrument::gpu()`, the `[gpu]` GPU opened once.
- **`siq-config`:** `[gpu]` `enabled`, `software`, `mosaic_vram_gb` (2).
- **`siq-preview`:** `Previews::pool()`, the frame-path pool shared.
- **`siq-storage`:** `DatasetKind::Mosaic`.
- **The web app's API** regenerated: `openapi.json` and `schema.d.ts` with the Thing.
- **`live_mosaic_sim`:** 6 × 6 fields of 2048 × 1536 stepped while live, the area's view and a 1:1
  view of a corner written as JPEGs, and the mosaic saved.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_stitch_live::{Viewport, View, view, Preparer, Prepared, PAGE_BYTES}`,
  `Canvas::render`; `siq_server::mosaic::{MosaicLive, MosaicBounds, MosaicStats, MosaicSnapshot}`,
  `Instrument::gpu`; `siq_config::model::GpuConfig`; `Previews::pool`; `DatasetKind::Mosaic`.
- **HTTP:** `/api/v1/mosaic_live/…`: the properties `enabled`, `viewport`, `canvas_scale`,
  `max_uncertainty_px` (writable), `bounds`, `px_per_um`, `stats`; the actions `clear` and
  `save_snapshot`; the stream `view`.
- **For P7.4:** the Mosaic page sets `viewport` from its pan and zoom, and places the camera's
  footprint and the stage on the stream's image with `px_per_um` and the viewport: a view pixel is
  `1 / (zoom × px_per_um)` µm. `bounds` is the minimap's box.

## Deviations

1. **Moving frames rarely paint by default.** Their pose's uncertainty is how far the stage went
   between readings, 0.2 s apart at 5 Hz: at the default 2 canvas pixels (M7's placement
   criterion), only below about 7 µm/s at 10×. Settled frames always paint, so a jog's stops, or
   moves field by field, fill the map. `max_uncertainty_px` trades accuracy for coverage while
   moving; faster stage polling would narrow the uncertainty itself.
2. **The view samples the nearest canvas pixel,** not a box: zoomed far out, it aliases. Pages
   kept at several detail levels would fix it, if navigation needs it.
3. **The window is the newest frame's,** with the display's automatic contrast: the whole view
   brightens or darkens with it, as the live view does.
4. **The canvas follows the frames' calibration:** a new calibration clears it, as its frames
   would land elsewhere.
5. **A snapshot raises no `datasets.created`:** the Thing answers with the dataset, and the catalog
   lists it.
6. **Grey only:** a channel's colour isn't applied to the view.

## Measurements

- **The mosaic against the specimen:** a 40 × 40 crop where the stage stopped correlates 0.870
  with the world sampled there, best at no offset of ±3 canvas pixels: placement within a canvas
  pixel.
- **The view against the canvas:** no pixel off by more than 1 level.
- **`live_mosaic_sim`** (release, RTX 4060): 36 fields of 2048 × 1536 in 15.7 s, the stage's moves
  and settling most of it; 84 frames painted, the last in 2.2 ms with its binning and upload; 58
  moving frames passed over; 145 frames published, 0 lost; 4 pages on the GPU, 268 MB of the 2 GB
  budget. The 1:1 view where four fields meet is coherent, its seams visible only as the frames'
  uncorrected vignetting.
