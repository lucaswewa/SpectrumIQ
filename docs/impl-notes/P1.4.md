# P1.4 — Synthetic specimen worlds (`siq-synth`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P1.4-synth`, from `main` at `fd66d2d`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **`World`:** `intensity(at, footprint)`, the in-focus intensity from 0 to 1, as a pure function
  of sample coordinates. Also `surface_z`, `density`, `beads(region)` and `info()`.
- **`sample(world, &PixelToStage, out)`** renders on any pixel grid. `grid(origin, scale)` is the
  axis-aligned one.
- **The worlds:**
  - `Cells`: ellipses with nuclei and texture;
  - `Grains`: Voronoi grains with exact bisector boundaries;
  - `HeightField`: a textured relief of known height;
  - `Beads`: a Gaussian slab with a bead list;
  - `ImageWorld`: PNG or TIFF, 8 or 16 bits, grey or colour, with a pyramid of 2 × 2 means.
- **Anti-aliasing by footprint:** fractal octaves fade, edges ramp, beads widen, and the image
  world blends pyramid levels.
- **Randomness:** stateless hashes of lattice points, a key per world and use, and a random
  rotation per noise octave.
- **`WorldInfo`:** a kind-tagged JSON description that rebuilds the world, and maps
  `siq-config`'s `[sim.world]`.
- **`write_ground_truth`:** `<name>.json`, plus `<name>.height.f32` (raw little-endian µm).
  `HeightMap::read` reads the heights back.

## Interfaces

- **API:** `siq_synth::{World, sample, grid, Bead, Cells, CellsParams, Grains, GrainsParams,
  HeightField, HeightFieldParams, Beads, BeadsParams, ImageWorld, ImageParams, WorldInfo,
  GroundTruth, HeightMap, write_ground_truth, SynthError}`.
- **P1.6:**
  - `sample` with the frame's `PixelToStage`, from `StageToPixel::for_frame(…).inverse()`;
  - `surface_z` for the defocus;
  - a coarser grid, whose footprint averages detail, when the defocus blur allows it;
  - parallel rendering over row bands, and the noiseless cache, both of which P1.6 brings.
- **P1.7 and P2.3:** `WorldInfo::from_config(&config.sim.world, config.sim.seed).build()`.
- **`siq-datagen` (P4.9, P12.4, P13.1):** `write_ground_truth` beside each dataset, and
  `WorldInfo` in its manifest.

## Deviations

1. **`sample(region: RectUm, scale, out)` became `sample(world, &PixelToStage, out)` plus
   `grid(origin, scale)`,** so that frames with a rotated or flipped calibration render directly
   (ADR-0024).
2. **`intensity(at, footprint)` is what a world implements,** and `sample` is built on it.
3. **`surface_z(at)` and `density(at, z)` take a `Vec2Um` and return `Option`s.**
4. **Added:** `beads(region)`, `info()`, `WorldInfo::build` and `WorldInfo::from_config`.
5. **Performance work beyond the brief.**
   - *What happened:* the first renders took 200–800 ns per pixel.
   - *What changed:* searches are limited to what can reach a point, cells are only shaped when
     near, hashes are one mix per lattice point, and keys are made once per world.
   - *The result:* 67–133 ns per pixel.
6. **Grain boundaries were corrected while reviewing the renders.**
   - *The first formula,* half the difference of the two nearest distances, drew dark wedges
     where two seeds were close together.
   - *The bisector distance* fixed the wedges.
   - *A 3 × 3 search* missed a few boundaries; the 5 × 5 fallback fixed them.

## Measurements

**Counted lines:** **836**, below 1000 and the brief's ~900.

**Statistics, seeds 1–3:**

| Measure | Measured | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| Area covered by cells | 0.165–0.169 | about 0.17 |
| Share of grain boundaries | 0.052–0.056 | — |
| Beads in 300 × 300 µm | 519–576 | about 533 |

**Sampling time,** single-threaded, release build, 0.345 µm per pixel:

| World | Per pixel |
|---|---|
| `Cells` | 133 ns |
| `Grains` | 133 ns |
| `HeightField` | 67 ns |
| `Beads` | 83 ns |

**`render_world`, full run:** 2048 × 1536 pixels per world in 0.2–0.4 s each. The renders were
looked at, and the grain boundaries corrected as a result (deviation 6).

**New dependencies:** `png` 0.18.1, `tiff` 0.11.3 (deflate and LZW only), and their crates (see
`docs/soup.md`).

- `crc32fast` was pinned to 1.5.2, because 1.5.3 was 3 days old.
- `flate2` was pinned to 1.1.9, so that `png` and `tiff` share one `miniz_oxide`.

## Follow-ups

  measurements above.
- **Optional:** coherent row sampling, if P1.6 measures that the worlds limit the moving frame
