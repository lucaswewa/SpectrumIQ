# P6.6 — Scan setup UI — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P6.6-scan-setup`, from `main` at `b6c9155`
- **Date:** 2026-10-26

## Summary

- **The Scan page** (ADR-0057), `/#/scan`, and `/#/dev/scan` against a fake instrument:
  - **the stage map,** an SVG in stage µm: the travel, the frame where the stage is, the saved
    positions, the region drawn (rectangle or polygon), the focus map's places, and the tiles
    planned, each its frame, in their order. The wheel zooms about the pointer;
  - **the form:** the objective in use, name, channels, overlap, order; the focus as it is, at one
    focus, on a focus map, or autofocused at each tile; and a z-stack at each tile;
  - **the estimate,** the server's, asked as the plan settles: tiles, frames, time, data, the
    disk's room, memory, and warnings; the gate's blockers (ADR-0051) and the form's problems
    first;
  - **Start,** when nothing blocks and the disk holds it; the run's progress below it.
- **The server:**
  - `workflow.estimate_tile_scan`, without the global lock: the planner's tiles and frame, the
    estimate with the stage's own kinematics, the disk, and warnings;
  - `workflow.field`: the frame's corners about where the stage is;
  - `Stage::kinematics()`, the GRBL driver's from `$11x` and `$12x`;
  - `scan::estimate` counts a z-stack's planes and focus moves, autofocus, and a focus map
    (`Focusing`); a focus move is 0.15 s, as measured on the simulator; `scan::frame`.
- **Saved positions** on the Live page are the server's now: saved, renamed, gone to and forgotten
  through the `workflow` Thing.

## Deviations

1. **No validator against the plan's JSON Schema at run time:** the TD's schema has no steps
   (ADR-0056), and one would be a new dependency. The form's checks mirror the engine's, the tests
   check its requests against the OpenAPI schema, and the server's 422 is shown as the reason.
2. **The run's progress on the Scan page,** until P6.7's monitor.
3. **The objective is shown, not chosen:** it is chosen on the Live page, with its parfocal
   confirmation (ADR-0039).
4. **The saved positions' move to the server,** a follow-up of P6.5.
5. **The estimate's kinematics and focusing,** a follow-up of P6.2.
