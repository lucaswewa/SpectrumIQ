# P6.1 — Workflow engine — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `cffdea0`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P6.1-engine`, from `main` at `217d4b0`
- **Date:** 2026-10-26

## Summary

- **`siq-workflow::engine`** (ADR-0052): one executor for every acquisition plan.
  - **`Plan`:** a name, steps and an error policy, serde with its JSON Schema. Device steps
    `move_to`, `focus`, `channel`, `snap` and `wait`; structure `repeat` (numbering `t`, `z` or
    `p` within it), `index` and `policy`.
  - **`run`:**
    - makes the dataset, with the plan, the instrument and the calibration in force in its
      manifest;
    - records each snap under the key of its indices and channel, a key once per run;
    - reports progress (done, total, ETA, step) and events;
    - pauses between steps, and stops when cancelled, abandoning a move but finishing a capture,
      then halts the stage and the focus;
    - applies each step's error policy: retry, skip, or the run's end.
  - **`RunControl`:** pause, resume and cancel, from anywhere.
- **`DeviceSet`:** the instrument as the workflows see it; `Instrument::devices` makes one in the
  server.
- **`routines::channel`:** putting a channel in place, moved from the server so that the engine
  and the `imaging` Thing share it.
- **`run_plan_sim`:** a small time-lapse of a multi-position acquisition on the simulated
  instrument.

## Interfaces

- **Public:**

  - `RoutineError::Channel`;
  - `siq_storage::DatasetKind::Sequence`; `Acquisition: Clone`; `Instrument::{devices, clock}`.
- **For P6.2–P6.4:** a tile scan, autofocus and a z-stack are new `Step` variants, and `count`
  and the runner learn them.
- **For P6.5:** the `workflow` Thing takes a `Plan`, runs it on `instrument.devices()` while it
  holds the global lock, and maps `RunControl` and `RunEvent` to its actions, properties and
  events.

## Deviations

1. **The tests run in real time, not under `tokio::time::pause`.** The simulated camera renders
   on a thread of its own, in real time; paused time would run past its waits. Mock motion
   arrives at once, and the six tests take about 3 s.
2. **An `index` step,** beside the brief's steps: without it, places reached by separate moves
   couldn't be numbered.
3. **A capture under way finishes when a run is cancelled;** a move is abandoned. Cutting off a
   capture would leave the camera armed for a frame no one takes.
4. **A run doesn't return the stage to its start:** a plan that wants it ends with a `move_to`.
5. **A key may be taken once:** found by the first draft of the pause test, whose three
   unnumbered snaps overwrote each other's file. A second snap at a key now fails, saying so.
