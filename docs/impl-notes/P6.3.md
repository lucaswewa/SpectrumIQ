# P6.3 — Autofocus and focus maps — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P6.3-autofocus`, from `main` at `489f317`
- **Date:** 2026-10-26

## Summary

- **`siq-workflow::autofocus`** (ADR-0054):
  - **`autofocus`:** a coarse sweep of 11 frames over 50 µm, binned 2 × 2 at a quarter of the
    exposure, then a fine one of 13 frames over ±1.5 coarse steps about the coarse estimate, at
    the camera's own settings;
  - **sharpness** is Tenengrad over the frame's middle three quarters;
  - **each estimate** is the vertex of a Gaussian fitted to the frames above half the sweep's peak;
  - **nothing to focus on:** a rise under a fifth of the peak finds no focus, and the drive goes
    back;
  - **it returns** the focus, the curve and the rise. The camera is put back after, whatever
    happened.
- **Focus surfaces** (`fit_surface`):
  - a plane by least squares, with places more than three robust deviations off it set aside;
  - or a thin-plate spline through every place;
  - the residuals: the plane's at each place, the spline's with the place left out.
- **The engine's steps:**
  - `autofocus`, which fails with nothing to focus on;
  - `focus_map`, which makes the surface in force, leaving out the places with nothing to focus
    on;
  - `focus_on_surface`;
  - a tile scan's `focus`: `none`, `fixed`, `surface` or `autofocus`, at each tile;
  - the maps go in `layout.json` beside the grids.
- **`siq-sim`:**
  - **`SimFocus`:** a focus drive that arrives at once and moves the true focus;
  - **the GRBL emulator** lands on each move's target, and draws a random error only for the
    axes that moved.
- **`autofocus_sim`:** five places on a gentle relief, each sweep's curve printed, the focus
  against the truth, and a spline's residuals.

## Deviations

1. **Stepped sweeps, a `snap` at each step, not a `Bounded` fan-out tap on the stream.** The
   focus drive's trait has no speed, so a streamed frame's z could only be guessed by time.
   `microscope`'s ADR-0022 measured that bias at up to a frame's worth of z.
2. **The fast mode is binning, for the coarse sweep only.** No ROI: the measure uses the frame's
   middle three quarters. The fine sweep is at full resolution, because binned it followed the
   noise.
3. **The fine search is a second sweep with a Gaussian fit,** not a parabola about the coarse peak
   or golden section (ADR-0054's alternatives).
4. **A focus map's places are given, not laid on a grid:** the scan setup page (P6.6) lays them,
   over the region it shows.
5. **Two fixes to the simulator's stage, outside `siq-workflow`.** A new random error for every
   axis at every move moved z by about 0.5 µm whenever x or y moved. And a move's landing was
   computed along its profile, a rounding error from its target, so z counted as having moved
   after an overshoot. Both put the focus found at a place off at the tile. Before the fixes, the
   map scan's worst tile was 2.17 µm out against a limit of 1.84; after, 0.97.

## Measurements

- **Autofocus error,** grains on a surface tilted by 0.03 and curved, at 256 × 192:
  - at the map's nine places, from five starts each, −1.03 to +0.31 µm;
  - within 0.08 µm of each other at each place: the error is the place's, the tilt across the
    frame (3–7 µm over 96 µm) and where the grains are in it.
- **On a gentle relief at 512 × 384** (`autofocus_sim`, release): within 0.08 µm at five places
  from up to 22 µm out, 4.9 s an autofocus. On the height field's default relief (±40 µm, down
  to a 50 µm period) at 1024 × 768, up to 6.4 µm from the centre's focus: the relief across the
  frame is several depths of field.
- **The map scan:** the worst tile 0.97 µm out, against 1.84.
- **Confidence:** a blank field rises by 7.9 % from every start; grains and cells by 78–98 %.

## Follow-ups

- **P6.5:** in the server's simulator, the specimen is in focus at the focus drive's 0, the
  bottom of the example dialect's travel (0 to 12 mm). An autofocus there sweeps only above
  focus. The simulated focal plane should sit within the travel, before autofocus is an action.
- **P6.6:** the scan setup page lays the focus map and shows its residuals.
- **Later:** a looping autofocus that follows a focus outside its range, if a real slide needs
  it.
- **Later:** an autofocus that a cancel stops between frames, putting the camera back. Today a
  cancel waits for it to finish, about 5 s on the simulator.
