# P5.3 — Shading correction — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P5.3-shading`, from `main` at `103f3be`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **`siq-calib::shading`** (ADR-0050):
  - `Accumulator`, the frames' sum, for a dark's or a flat's mean;
  - `FlatField`, a polynomial surface of degree 6 fitted by least squares to a flat less its dark,
    relative to its mean, and its gains for any frame's or preview's geometry;
  - `Estimator`, the retrospective flat (BaSiC, simplified): the per-cell median of tiles each
    shrunk and divided by its mean, then fitted;
  - `FlatFieldView`, the correction of frames of one geometry as they are read;
  - `ShadingRecord` and `ShadingRegistry`, a dark (with its `.npy`) or a flat per objective and
    channel.
- **The registry is generic** (`Registry<R: Versioned>`): the stage–camera calibrations and the
  shading share it, with any data kept beside a record.
- **`siq-workflow::routines::shading`:** the dark with every light off and back on; the flat at
  nine places, out of focus or of a blank slide, the stage and focus put back.
- **`siq-jobs::kinds::ShadingJob`:** a dataset's tiles' flat, as a background job.
- **The previews' correction** (`siq-preview`), turned on by `display.flat_field`; and the
  `calibration` Thing's `capture_dark`, `capture_flat` and `estimate_shading`, with `status` per
  objective and channel.
- **`estimate_flat`:** a `siq-datagen` tile scan's flat, against the vignetting it was rendered
  with.

## Deviations

1. **The flat is smoothed by its form, a polynomial of degree 6, not by a Gaussian low-pass.** A
   blur that holds the edges beyond the image pulls a curved fall-off toward the centre's level at
   the corners. The polynomial is also what the record keeps: no gain map on disk.
2. **The retrospective estimate is the per-cell median of normalised tiles, fitted**, without
   BaSiC's low-rank decomposition. The darkfield isn't estimated: the dark level is given or in
   force. The brief allows it.
3. **The flat capture defaults to a blank slide (no defocus).** The test takes the flat 50 µm out
   of focus on a blank slide. On the cells world, its background's ±5 % over 200 µm would stay in
   a defocused flat.
4. **`status` is computed on each read,** not a stored property: a job's flat shows as soon as the
   job ends.
5. **`Renderer::show` takes the shading** (or `None`), so the snap's JPEG and the tests pass
   `None`.
