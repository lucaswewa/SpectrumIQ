# P6.2 — Tile scan planner and step — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `489f317`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P6.2-tile-scan`, from `main` at `cffdea0`
- **Date:** 2026-10-26

## Summary

- **`siq-workflow::scan`** (ADR-0053), pure:
  - **`plan`:** a region (a rectangle or a polygon, in stage µm), the calibration `L`, the frame,
    the overlap and an order give a `Grid` of tiles;
  - **the grid runs along the camera's axes,** so neighbours overlap by exactly the share asked;
  - **the tiles kept** are those whose frame meets the region;
  - **the orders:** snake, raster, nearest.
- **`estimate`:** moves at the stage's speed and acceleration with a driver overhead, settling,
  channel switches, exposures and readouts; the bytes, and whether the disk holds them.
- **The engine's `tile_scan` step:** made into `index`, `move_to`, `wait`, `channel` and `snap`
  steps when a run starts, by the calibration in force and the camera's region, which it needs.
  The grid goes in the dataset's `layout.json`, and the dataset is of the kind `tile_scan`.
- **`tilescan_sim`:** a 5 × 5 scan in two channels on the simulated instrument, estimated, then
  run.

## Interfaces

- **Public:**
  - `siq_workflow::scan::{Region, Order, Tile, Grid, plan, ScanError, MAX_TILES, Timing,
    Estimate, estimate}`;
  - `siq_workflow::engine::{Step::TileScan, resolve, LAYOUT}`.
- **For P6.3:** the tile scan's `focus` becomes a focus surface's z at each tile.
- **For P6.6:** the scan setup page plans with `scan::plan` and estimates with `scan::estimate`
  (through the `workflow` Thing, P6.5), showing the grid and the estimate before Start.
- **For M8:** stitching reads `layout.json` for each tile's planned position and grid place.

## Deviations

1. **The grid runs along the camera's axes,** not the stage's: neighbours then overlap by exactly
   the share asked in the image.
2. **No RAM check:** a scan's frames pass through the fixed frame pool to the disk, so its RAM
   doesn't grow with it. The disk check is `Estimate::fits`.
3. **The estimate's timings are given, with the simulator's as defaults:** the device traits
   don't expose speeds or accelerations. The server will pass the configured stage's (P6.5).
