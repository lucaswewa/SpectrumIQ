# P4.7 — `datasets` Thing and processing on snap — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `1565ece`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P4.7-datasets`, from `main` at `ab59822`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **The snap pipeline** (ADR-0041, amended), behind `datasets.snap_to_dataset(channels?, name?)`:
  1. **The frames are recorded raw:**
     - a dataset of kind `snap` is made, with the instrument's configuration as its snapshot;
     - the frame recorder runs on a lossless subscriber of the frames marked to save;
     - a frame is snapped in each channel given, each channel applied first, or one in the channel
       in use;
     - frame `c` is keyed `c`.
  2. **`siq_jobs::kinds::ZarrFromRawJob`** writes them as level 0 of `zarr/snap.ome.zarr`, a
     channel each:
     - named and coloured as their channels;
     - each in its frame's auto-contrast window, from the histogram taken at the snap;
     - the pixel size from the sensor, binning and objective;
     - the origin from the stage's position.
  3. **The pyramid job** is submitted.

  The action gives the dataset's entry, its image, and the pyramid's job, and emits `created`.
- **The `datasets` Thing:**
  - `summary(offset, limit)`, a page of the catalog, newest first;
  - `export_ome_tiff` and `export_dzi`, as background jobs;
  - `delete`, refused while a job is queued or running on the dataset;
  - the event `created`.

  Only `snap_to_dataset` takes the global lock.
- **Routes:**
  - `GET /api/siq/data/{dataset}/manifest`;
  - `GET /api/siq/data/{dataset}/thumb.jpg`: the first image's first plane, its channels
    composited in their colours, at the first level within 256 pixels.
- **Underneath:**
  - `ZarrImage::write_plane`: a whole plane, its chunks in parallel;
  - `Catalog::delete`: the folder, then the entry;
  - `siq_pyramid::tiles::{composite, thumbnail}`.
- **`examples/python/snap_and_fetch_tile.py`:** snaps into a dataset, waits for the pyramid in the
  manifest's history, then saves the centre tile of the level fitting 1920 pixels, and the
  thumbnail, with the time each step took.
- **`test.http`** gains the `datasets` and `jobs` Things and the data routes: a dataset made, its
  manifest, thumbnail, descriptor, tiles, probe, exports and deletion.
- **Architecture §6.1 and §6.2** are updated, and the web app's OpenAPI document and types are
  regenerated.

## Deviations

1. **`summary` is a paged action, not a property:** teta-wot properties take no parameters.
   Architecture §6.1 is updated.
2. **The raw-to-Zarr step is a job,** `zarr_from_raw`, not code in the action. It is recorded in
   the history beside the pyramid, admitted within the budget, and run on the jobs' threads. The
   action waits for it, then submits the pyramid.
3. **Frames must be 16-bit:** Mono8 snaps are refused for now. The cameras planned deliver 12 to 14
   bits.
4. **The pixel size and origin are approximations:**
   - the sensor's pitch, binned, through the objective;
   - the stage's position, taken as the frame's centre.

   Both wait for the stage–camera calibration (M5).

- **`snap_and_fetch_tile.py`** ran against the release service on `config/sim.toml`, whose camera
  is 11 648 × 8742 Mono14 (101.8 MP).

    - the dataset was made in 1.05 s;

  16383). That's a known limitation of the simulator (siq-server's README).
