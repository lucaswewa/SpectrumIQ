# P8.1 — Overlap graph and pairwise registration — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P8.1-pairs`, from `main` at `756ff1e`
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Summary

- **`siq-stitch`,** a new crate (L3), ADR-0061:
  - **`Mosaic::place`:** each frame's tile at `L·P / binning − size / 2`, on the camera's pixel
    grid, from its pose and the calibration; the frames must share a binning;
  - **`Mosaic::overlaps`:** the pairs overlapping by 32 pixels each way over 5 % of a tile, by a
    sweep along x;
  - **`pairs::register`:** each pair's nominal overlap, as whole pixels of each tile,
    flat-corrected if a flat is given, registered within the stage's reach, into an `Edge` of the
    measured offset, its energy, margin, correlation and overlap, and whether it is trusted;
  - **`bytes_per_pair`** and contiguous groups of pairs, at most `threads` at once;
  - **`TileSource`:** a dataset's frames mapped read-only (`MappedTiles`), or images in memory.
- **`siq-register`:** `Options::reach`, the largest shift weighed each way, and
  `RegisterError::Unreached`.
- **`pairs_on_dataset`:** a `siq-datagen` scan of 5 × 4 tiles of 2048 × 1536, or a dataset of
  yours, its pairs registered and set against the truth.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_stitch::{Mosaic, Tile, TileSource, MappedTiles, Edge, PairOptions,
  StitchError, pairs::{register, bytes_per_pair}}`; `siq_register::Options::reach`,
  `RegisterError::Unreached`.
- **For P8.2:** the solver takes the mosaic's nominal corners and the trusted edges' offsets,
  weighted by their confidence, with the stage's σ as each tile's prior.
- **For P8.4:** the stitch job estimates `threads × bytes_per_pair`, places the tiles through the
  dataset's calibration snapshot, and registers its reference channel and plane.

## Deviations

1. **Coarse to fine is the registrar's:** it shrinks overlaps to 512 pixels a side for the search,
   rather than the brief's fixed 4 ×, then refines at full resolution. The search window is the
   new `reach`.
2. **Corners are left out** of the graph by a minimum share of a tile's area (5 %): a minimum each
   way can't tell them from sides, as a corner passes whenever both kinds of side do.
3. **`Edge` also carries** the margin and `confident`, so that the solver and the QA (P8.4) read
   one trust.
4. **The thresholds rose** from the brief's sketch to an energy of 300 and a quarter of the overlap
   in common: at 100, a sliver of sparse cells was trusted 32 pixels off (see Measurements).
5. **The flat is the caller's:** a dataset doesn't record the flat in force; the stitch job (P8.4)
   takes it from the shading registry, or a tile scan's own estimate.

## Measurements

- **Ground truth, 3 × 3 over grains:** 0.064 px RMS, 0.126 px at worst.
- **`pairs_on_dataset`** (release, 16 threads): 20 tiles of 2048 × 1536 over cells at 15 %
  overlap, the stage 5 µm off; 31 pairs registered in 51 ms, all trusted, 20 MB each at most;
  0.025 px RMS of the truth, 0.054 at worst, where the stage's own offsets were 22.7 px RMS off.
- **Memory:** 10.2 MB held for a pair of 1024 × 768 tiles at 30 % overlap, against 16.4 MB
  estimated; 20.2 MB two at a time.
- **On cells,** a side whose strips share one partial cell registers with an energy of 13: not
  trusted, rightly.
- **Smoke mode,** 3 × 3 tiles of 512 × 384 over cells: at an energy of 100, a 57-pixel strip was
  trusted 32 pixels off (energy 129, correlation 0.62), 9.2 px RMS over the pairs; at 300, 10 of
  12 pairs are trusted, within 0.21 px RMS and 0.61 at worst. At full size, every side's energy
  is over 8000.
