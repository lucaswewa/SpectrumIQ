# P5.1 — Phase correlation — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P5.1-register`, from `main` at `59493eb`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **`siq-register`** (L1, ADR-0048): `Registrar::register(a, b)`, the shift of `b`'s content
  from `a`'s, sub-pixel, with its confidence.
  - **The search:**
    - Tukey-tapered images (a fifth of each side, by default), transformed by `realfft` (rows)
      and `rustfft` (columns), at any size;
    - the normalised cross-power spectrum, low-passed to a Gaussian peak of one pixel;
    - images over 512 a side are searched shrunk by block means.
  - **Candidates:**
    - the 8 highest peaks, each with its four wrap hypotheses;
    - within a peak, the overlap's normalised cross-correlation chooses;
    - across peaks, the peak's height times that correlation wins.
  - **The refinement:**
    - Hann-tapered windows of the overlap that show the same content, at full resolution, with
      sides of factors 2, 3 and 5;
    - repeated until the residual is under half a pixel.
  - **Sub-pixel:** a Gaussian through the peak and its neighbours, by default; a parabola, or
    Foroosh.
  - **Confidence:** the search's PCE, the refinement's peak margin, and the overlap's correlation.
  - **FFT plans** are cached per size in the registrar.
- **`register_pair`:** simulated camera frames, moved by known amounts.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_register::{Registrar, Options, Window, Subpixel, Registration, RegisterError,
  MIN_SIZE}`.
- **For P5.2:** the calibration routine registers each frame after a move against the first. Moving
  the stage by `m` gives a shift of `−m` over the pixel size with the axes aligned.
  `siq-calib` fits the affine from the moves and shifts.
- **For M8:** stitching registers the overlap crops of neighbouring tiles, and weighs `pce` and
  `margin`.

## Deviations

1. **A Gaussian sub-pixel fit, on a low-passed peak,** is the default, beside the brief's parabola
   and Foroosh. On the sharp whitened peak, the parabola was up to 0.27 px off. The low-pass makes
   the peak Gaussian, and a Gaussian fit exact (ADR-0048).
2. **The search's taper is Tukey, and the refinement's Hann.** Hann over the whole images tapered a
   20 % overlap at an edge away; refining in windows of the same content removes the tapers' pull
   toward zero.
3. **A refinement stage at every size,** not only after a coarse search: each image tapered in its
   own coordinates biases the peak, by 0.27 px at a 40-pixel shift.
4. **`pce` comes from the search, `margin` and `ncc` from the refinement.** There, a periodic
   pattern's copies stand as high as its true peak (margin 0.14), and unrelated images' PCE stays
   low (33).
5. **The low-overlap test uses the grains world, on 512 × 512:** a 20 % strip of sparse cells,
   51 pixels on 256, held no peak to find. Tiles' overlaps are hundreds of pixels wide, and
   textured.
