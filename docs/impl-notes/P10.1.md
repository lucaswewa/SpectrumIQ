## Summary

- **The owner's answers,** asked as M10 began, are in [M10's amendment](../plan/milestones/M10-hardware-bringup.md#amendment-p101-2026-10-29-the-owners-answers-and-the-colour-phases):
  - the Adimec D-103A12c on this PC is the instrument's camera, its frames raw Bayer;
  - the agent may run the hardware tests, putting back what it changes;
  - the stage and the focus drive stay simulated for now;
  - P10.1 to P10.4 are in scope.

  Two colour phases are added, P10.6 and P10.7.
- **`siq-euresys`** (new, L2): `EuresysCamera<G: Grabber>`, a `Camera` over eGrabber's `EGrabber`
  (with `sdk`) or the tests' fake (ADR-0070).
  - Every pool slot is a grabber buffer, filled by DMA only while the camera holds it, and
    requeued once its last lease drops.
  - One thread pops with a 20 ms tick, and steps aside for a caller that wants the grabber.
  - Frames that are incomplete or of the wrong size are lost (`lost_device`); underruns are
    `lost_no_slot`.
  - Teardown is ordered (stop, revoke, then the slots back), also when the thread panics.
- **`FeatureMap`:** a camera model's features, where they differ from SFNC's, in
  `config/cameras/adimec-d103a12c.toml`. The Adimec's software trigger is the grabber's camera
  controller's `StartCycle`.
- **Raw Bayer frames:** `siq_types::Cfa`, in `CameraCapabilities` and in each `FrameMeta`, shifted
  by a region's odd offsets.
- **The service:** `siq-server`'s and `spectrumiq-server`'s `euresys` feature builds
  `[camera] kind = "euresys"`, with the pool locked in RAM.
- **Examples:**
  - `eg_live`: the frame rate, the frames lost and the timestamps' jitter;
  - `eg_features`: a module's features for writing a map, and `--save` and `--restore` of the
    camera's settings around an experiment.

- **For P10.6 and P10.7:** each frame's `cfa`.

## Deviations

1. **The camera's frames stay raw Bayer,** where the brief assumed `PixelFormat = Mono16`. This is
   the owner's answer. `PixelFormat` stays the samples' depth, and `Cfa` the pattern.
2. **The Adimec isn't SFNC in its triggering and gain.** The map's trigger modes set any module's
   features, so a software trigger goes through the grabber's camera controller. The camera's own
   `TriggerSoftware` made no frame.
3. **The grabber's camera controller** (`CameraControlMethod`) is part of the trigger modes:
   `NC` free-running, `RC` triggered.
4. **A fair turn at the grabber:** with a plain mutex, a second software trigger waited forever
   on the camera, as the thread retook the lock at once. The fake tests didn't show it, and now
   test ten triggers while the thread pops.
5. **The period, set before the exposure and after it:** the camera shortened a longer exposure to
   fit its old period (350 ms became 79 ms).
6. **The camera's settings are put back raw by the tests and the examples,** as `eg_fps` does,
   from what they read first. Putting them back through the camera would recompute the frame
   period, not restore it.
7. **Not yet:** the thread's priority (P10.2), and the Playlink smoke test of the brief.
   P2.5's covers the shim, and the fake grabber covers the camera's logic.
