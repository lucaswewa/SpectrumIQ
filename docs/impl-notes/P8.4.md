# P8.4 — Stitch job, auto-stitch and QA UI — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `3c8045c`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P8.4-stitch-job`, from `main` at `a66f886`
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Summary

- **`siq-jobs::kinds::StitchJob`** (ADR-0064): the reference plane's pairs registered and solved,
  every frame composited at its tile's corner by its position, a channel at a time through its own
  flat, the pyramid built, and `qa/<image>.json` written; the report's summary as the job's
  results. `CompositeJob` takes its shading by channel, and its run is shared as `compose`.
- **Results in the history:** `JobOutput::results`, written by the scheduler into the record's new
  `ProcessingRecord::results` when the job ends.
- **`siq-stitch::Report`:** each tile's corner, size, move, worst residual and component; each
  pair's residual, energy, correlation, trust and use; a `Summary`. `StitchOptions` gathers the
  pairs', the solve's and the composite's options.
- **`siq-server`:**
  - `jobs.stitch(dataset, image?, options?)`, made from the dataset's calibration snapshot, the
    channels' names and colours, and each channel's dark and flat in force;
  - `jobs.auto_stitch`, on at start, and the hook that stitches each finished tile scan into
    `stitched` at background priority;
  - `GET /api/siq/data/{dataset}/{image}/overlays`: the report as two overlay layers, tile
    outlines and toned residual lines.
- **The web app:**
  - the Jobs page: running jobs with their progress and message, waiting ones, Cancel, and
    auto-stitch;
  - Stitch… on the Datasets page's details, and its options dialog;
  - the Viewer shows a stitched image's overlays;
  - overlay shapes take a `tone`, coloured by the theme.
- **`examples/python/stitch_dataset.py`;** `test.http` gains the calibration, stitching and live
  mosaic requests.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_jobs::kinds::StitchJob`, `JobOutput::results`; `CompositeJob::shading` is now
  by channel id; `siq_storage::ProcessingRecord::results`; `siq_stitch::{Report, Summary,
  StitchOptions, report::{TileQa, EdgeQa}}`.
- **HTTP:** `jobs.stitch`, `jobs.auto_stitch`; the `overlays` data route.
- **Web:** `useJobsStore`, `JobsPage`, `StitchDialog`; `Shape.tone`.
- **For P8.5:** the accuracy suite and the 500 MP benchmark run the same job, and tune its
  defaults.

## Deviations

1. **No separate overlay rendering endpoint for images:** the overlays are vector layers the viewer
   draws (ADR-0009), from the report; the brief's "edge and residual colours" are tones.
2. **Auto-stitch is a property, not configuration:** on at every start (ADR-0064).
3. **The test's simulator images grains:** on 256 × 192 tiles, the simulator's sparse cells left
   two of six pairs trusted, rightly, and the test is of the pipeline, not the specimen.
4. **`test.http` also gains** the live mosaic's requests, missed in P7.3, and the calibration's,
   an item open since M5.
