# P4.9 — Synthetic dataset generator — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `59493eb`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P4.9-datagen`, from `main` at `bf1df03`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **`apps/siq-datagen`** (ADR-0047): a library of generators, and the CLI around it.
  - **`Generator`** (`command`, `generate`) and **`GENERATORS`:** each generator is a subcommand, a
    `clap::Args` struct augmented at run time. M12 and M13 add theirs to the list.
  - **`--out`** (the data root, `data` by default), **`--seed`** (1) and **`--smoke`** apply to
    every generator.
  - **The shared parts:**
    - `Camera`: the simulated instrument's, 0.376 µm a pixel, with `render` (through
      `siq_sim::optics::form_image`) and `digitize` (the simulator's noise, for a signal made
      elsewhere);
    - `dataset`: a `synthetic` dataset with its plan and its `truth/` folder;
    - `record`, `write_stack` (the planes as OME-Zarr, with the pyramid) and `write_truth`;
    - a seeded normal for the stage's errors.
- **`tilescan`:**
  - cells or grains, 8 × 7 tiles of 4096 × 3072 at 15 % overlap by default;
  - each frame rendered at its true pose (nominal plus a 2 µm normal error per axis) and recorded
    with the nominal one;
  - `truth/tilescan.json`: every tile's nominal and true pose and error;
  - `zarr/truth.ome.zarr`: the ideal mosaic, the world sampled chunk by chunk in parallel, with
    its pyramid. 533 MP by default.
- **`zstack`:**
  - a height field at 41 focus positions 2 µm apart (2048 × 1536);
  - the true height of every pixel;
  - the stack as OME-Zarr.
- **`beads`:**
  - a bead volume through a 3D Gaussian PSF, made here, each bead blurred by its own defocus and
    keeping its light;
  - then the simulator's noise;
  - every bead as truth;
  - the stack as OME-Zarr.
- **`siq_storage::write_frame`:** a frame made without the camera, written as the recorder
  writes one.

## Interfaces

- **Public:**
  - `siq_datagen::{Generator, GENERATORS, Output, Camera, Frame, dataset, write_truth, key,
    record, write_stack, gaussian, um}`;
  - each generator's `…Args` and its function (`tilescan::tilescan`, `zstack::zstack`,
    `beads::beads`);
  - `zstack::focus_positions`.
- **`siq_storage::write_frame`.**
- **For M8:**
  - `truth/tilescan.json`'s true poses, and `zarr/truth.ome.zarr`, are what stitching is measured
    against (REQ-STI-004);
  - the tiles are in `raw/`, keyed by `p`, with their nominal poses.
- **For M11 and M12:** `zstack`'s `world.height.f32`, and `beads`' bead list.

## Deviations

1. **The bead stacks have their own PSF:** the simulator treats a volume as flat, so every bead
   would share one defocus. Each bead is drawn through a Gaussian at its own defocus, then
   digitized by the simulator's noise model.
2. **The tile scan writes the ideal mosaic as well:** the milestone's 500 MP mosaic to browse, and
   M8's reference. `--no-mosaic` leaves it out.
3. **No example target:** the brief's example is the CLI, `cargo run -p siq-datagen -- tilescan
   --smoke`. A test runs the built binary.

**Browsing the 533 MP mosaic:** the release service ran on the generated data root with the web
build, and Edge was driven by Playwright.

- **Opening it:** the mosaic was whole in 1.0 s, 57 tiles.
- **Zooming in, twelve steps of 1.5× down to 1:1:** each step that needed tiles had them all in
  0.7 to 1.5 s, the length of OpenSeadragon's zoom animation, with 36 to 56 tiles a step.
- **The tiles:** 261 in all, each 2 ms at the median, 3 ms at the 95th percentile, and 4 ms at
  most, from request to response.
- **At 1:1** the cells were crisp. The probe read the true values. No page errors.
