# P4.4 — Pyramid builder — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P4.4-pyramid`, from `main` at `c557b1a`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **A new crate, `siq-pyramid`** (L3 Processing), per ADR-0044:
  - **`build(image, top, control)`:**
    - adds the levels that `top` needs (`levels_for`: halving until both sides fit, 256 by
      default);
    - builds each level from the one below, a chunk row at a time, its chunks and planes in
      parallel on the calling rayon pool;
    - each chunk is the 2 × 2 mean of exactly the four chunks below it, read once and written
      once;
    - reports progress by chunks, and stops when `control` says so.
  - **`mean_2x2`:** the filter, the edges included. `Mean` is implemented for `u16` (rounded to
    the nearest) and `f32` (exact).
  - **`estimate(info, threads)`:** the RAM at the peak.
  - **`Control`:** progress and cancellation, without the job system, which is a layer above.
- **`siq-storage`:** `ZarrImage::set_levels(n)` adds empty levels, and rewrites the metadata to
  list them. `create`'s group and level code is shared with it.
- **`siq-jobs`:** `kinds::PyramidJob`.
  - **Its estimate** comes from the image.
  - **It runs** the builder on the jobs' pool, mapping `cancelled` and `progress` to its context.
  - **Its output** is the image, as a path in its dataset.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_pyramid::{build, levels_for, estimate, mean_2x2, Control, Mean, PyramidError,
  TOP}`, `siq_storage::zarr::ZarrImage::set_levels` and `siq_jobs::kinds::PyramidJob`.
- **For P4.5:** every image has its levels down to about a tile, as the DZI geometry needs.
- **For P4.7:** a snap's pipeline submits a `PyramidJob` after writing level 0.

## Deviations

1. **The job lives in `siq_jobs::kinds`,** not in `siq-pyramid`. The layering forbids processing
   (L3) to use the job system (L4); the adapter is 67 lines.
2. **Memory is bounded per chunk, not per chunk row.** Each task holds its four source chunks and
   its result. That is under the brief's three chunk rows, and doesn't grow with the image's
   width.

- **`cargo xtask ci` passes.**
- **The memory test:** 8.4 MB at the peak, against a bound of 12.6 MB (three chunk rows) and the
  image's 32 MB.
- **`build_pyramid`** (release, this PC's 16 threads and NVMe drive):
  - a 20 000 × 20 000 `u16` image, 800 MB of pixels: level 0's 1600 chunks written in 1.5 s;
  - levels 1 to 7, down to 157 × 157, built in 1.3 s;
  - the process's peak working set 84 MB;
  - the top level's mean 7654, as the specimen's.
