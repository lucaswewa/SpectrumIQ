# P8.5 — Ground-truth validation and benchmarks — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `d2d583e`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P8.5-validation-benchmarks`, from `main` at `3c8045c`
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Summary

- **The accuracy suite** (`siq-stitch` `tests/accuracy.rs`): `siq-datagen` scans registered and
  solved, each tile placed within 0.3 px RMS and 1 px at worst of the truth.
  - In CI: three small scans, over grains and cells, one in a twentieth of the light.
  - With `--release --ignored`: a matrix of 18 scans of 2048 × 1536 tiles. It holds the exit
    criteria's scans (3 × 3, 5 × 5 and 10 × 10 at 15 % and 5 µm, over grains and over cells), and
    each of a 5 × 5 scan's overlap, stage error and light either way.
- **The 500 MP benchmark** (`siq-jobs` `bench_stitch_500mp`): 8 × 7 tiles of 4096 × 3072 generated,
  their flat estimated, then the stitch job within the 14 GB budget. It reports each stage's wall
  and processors' time, its peak working set and private bytes, and the bytes written, and the
  tiles' placement against the truth. It fails outside the targets: 30 s from the flat to the
  pyramid, 3 GB of working set, and the accuracy targets.
- **The defaults tuned** (`siq-stitch` `tune_stitch`):
  - the search now correlates up to 1024 pixels a side, through the new
    `PairOptions::search_px`, where it took the registrar's 512;
  - the reach (4 σ), Huber's k (1.345) and the feather (128 px) stay, measured
    (ADR-0061, ADR-0062, ADR-0063, amended).
- **The solve weighs each edge by its correlation,** up to 0.95 (ADR-0062, amended): a seed sweep
  found a weak false match beating a tile's true one.
- **Supporting changes:**
  - `siq-buffer::process_usage`: the process's processors' time and the bytes it read and wrote;
  - `siq-datagen`'s tile scan `--light`: the noise level;
  - the composite job logs `building the pyramid`.

## Interfaces

- **Public:**
  - `siq_stitch::pairs::PairOptions::search_px` and `pairs::search_bytes`;
  - `pairs::bytes_per_pair(&mosaic, &pairs, &options)` now takes the options;
  - `siq_buffer::{ProcessUsage, process_usage}`;
  - `siq_datagen::tilescan::TileScanArgs::light`.
- **HTTP:** `jobs.stitch`'s options take `pairs.search_px`; the OpenAPI document and the web's types
  are regenerated.
- **Behaviour:** the solve's edges weigh by their correlation; the stitch job's RAM estimate follows
  the search's size, 50 MB a pair for its FFTs, where it was 12.6 MB.
- **For P8.6:** the feature-based fallback can take the edges `tune_stitch` and the accuracy suite
  leave untrusted, the dim sparse cells' 5 of 40, and the narrow strips of small tiles.

## Deviations

1. **The solve changed, not only its defaults.** The tuning swept seeds as well as options, and in
   2 of 30 small scans a weak false match outweighed a tile's true one. The correlation weights fix
   both, kept within 0.95 so that a confident false edge can't outvote two true ones (ADR-0062,
   amended; `tests/solve.rs`: `where_a_tile_s_two_edges_disagree_the_better_match_wins`).
2. **The coarse factor is tuned as the search's size:** the registrar shrinks an overlap by the
   factor that brings it to `search_px`, and refines in windows of up to that. The size sets both
   the factor and the refinement's windows, and the windows count most.
3. **Disk I/O:** the tiles are read mapped, and the process's I/O counters don't count page faults.
   The benchmark reports the bytes written, and gives the tiles' size as read. The frames were in
   the system's cache, as a just-finished scan's are; a cold read adds its time at the disk's
   speed.
4. **The noise levels are the light** (`--light`, a share of the camera's flux): the simulator's
   shot noise grows as the light falls, as a sample's does.
5. **The benchmark's peak RAM is the stitch's**, sampled every 10 ms through its stages, not the
   process's peak. The process's peak is 4.3 GB, and includes the generator rendering the scan.
6. **`tune_stitch` registers through the simulator's flat,** as the service registers through the
   calibrated flat in force, not through one estimated from the scan being tuned.

## Follow-ups

- **Narrow strips:** the registrar's refinement can be a pixel off in a common strip of 58 pixels
  over a smooth specimen. Wider overlaps avoid it; P8.6's features, or a refinement that weighs the
  strip's length, could catch it.
- **A cold-cache benchmark,** on the instrument's own disk, at bring-up (M10).
