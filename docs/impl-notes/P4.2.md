# P4.2 — OME-Zarr I/O — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `f971f74`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P4.2-zarr`, from `main` at `6b5c500`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **`siq_storage::zarr`** (ADR-0042): OME-Zarr images, NGFF 0.5 on Zarr v3, through `zarrs`
  0.23.14 with its `filesystem` and `zstd` features only.
  - **`ImageInfo`:**
    - the name and the `SampleType` (`U16`, `F32`);
    - level 0's `Size5` (`t`, `c`, `z`, `y`, `x`), the levels, and the chunk (512);
    - the pixel size and origin in µm (`z`, `y`, `x`);
    - a `ChannelInfo` per channel: label, colour, window.

    `size(level)` halves `y` and `x` per level, rounding up, and `chunks(level)` counts them.
  - **`ZarrImage::create`** writes the group's NGFF metadata and an empty array per level:
    - `tczyx`, chunks of 1 × 1 × 1 × 512 × 512;
    - `bytes` then `zstd` at level 3;
    - dimension names, and no `_zarrs` attribute.
  - **`ZarrImage::open`** reads the metadata back, refusing another NGFF version or other axes,
    and takes the type, size and chunk from level 0's array.
  - **`write_chunk`** stores one chunk from any thread, in any order. Edge chunks are given
    clipped and padded with zeros.
  - **`read_region`** reads any rectangle of a level, refusing one outside it, as a `Region`
    (its rectangle and its pixels, with `view()`).
  - **The metadata:**
    - each level's `scale` (× 2ˡ in `y` and `x`), and a `translation` that centres its pixels on
      level 0's;
    - `omero` channels, with colour and window.
- **`StorageError::Zarr`** carries `zarrs`' errors, with the image's path.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `zarr::{ZarrImage, ImageInfo, Size5, SampleType, ZarrSample, ChannelInfo, Region,
  NGFF_VERSION, CHUNK}`.
- **For P4.4:** `ImageInfo::levels` makes the levels' arrays at creation. The pyramid builder
  writes levels 1 and up with `write_chunk`, reading level 0 with `read_region`.
- **For P4.5:** `read_region` at a level is what a tile reads.

## Deviations

1. **No sharding.** It isn't needed at 400 chunks a 100 MP plane, and can come later behind the
   same API (ADR-0042).
2. **Fiji wasn't checked:** it isn't installed on this PC. P4.6 installs it for the OME-TIFF
   check, and tries the OME-Zarr then.
3. **napari was checked without its canvas.** The tools' shell runs in Windows' service session,
   which has no OpenGL, so napari's viewer can't start there. The reader napari calls for an
   `.ome.zarr` (napari-ome-zarr's) ran instead, and napari layers were made from what it gave.
   Only the drawing is left unchecked.
4. **Levels are made with the image,** not added later. P4.4 decides the count before building.

- **`cargo xtask ci` passes.**
- **`zarr_write_read`** (release, this PC's 16 threads): 3 channels of 6144 × 4096 u16, 288
  chunks.
  - 151 MB written in 0.21 s, 707 MB/s, and 35 MB on disk (4.3× smaller).
  - A 600 × 400 region read back in 1.7 ms, with no pixel wrong.
- **napari 0.9.2, napari-ome-zarr 0.11.0, ome-zarr 0.21.0, zarr 3.4.1.** These were installed in a
  virtual environment in the session's scratch folder, from PyPI releases before 2026-10-11.
  `zarr_write_read --out` wrote the image, and the reader napari uses gave:
  - three image layers: `synthetic: rings`, `synthetic: gradient`, `synthetic: checkers`;
  - each (1, 1, 4096, 6144), `uint16`, scale 0.376 µm in `y` and `x`;
  - colourmaps magenta, green and the custom blue, from `omero`, with contrast (0, 16000) from the
    windows;
  - pixels as written, for instance the gradient at (2048, 2048) is 6666, as `(⅓ + ½) / 2 ×
    16000`.

## Measurements

**Counted lines:** **399**, below 1000, against the brief's ~850: `zarr.rs` 393, `lib.rs` 6.

**Dependencies:**
- **`zarrs` 0.23.14** (2026-08-14) brings 44 crates new to the lockfile. Three were pinned back
  from versions under two weeks old: `zarrs_filesystem` to 0.3.13, `zstd-sys` to
  2.1.1+zstd.1.5.7, and `libz-sys` to 1.1.29.
- **Their licences** are MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, or choices including them.
- **`zstd-sys` compiles zstd's C code,** which ADR-0007 allows for OME-Zarr.
- **`libz-sys`** is an unused build-dependency of `zarrs`: zlib is compiled, and linked into
  nothing.
