# P1.5 — CPU image kernels (`siq-imgproc`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `652ae8b`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P1.5-imgproc`, from `main` at `8713409`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **Correction:** `correct` and `correct_in_place`: `(raw − dark) × gain`, rounded and clamped to
  the format's maximum, with an optional `u16` dark frame and an optional `f32` gain map.
- **Resampling:**
  - `downsample_area` to any smaller size;
  - `bin` by any factors, as a sum or a mean;
  - `copy`, which also crops;
  - `gaussian_blur`, separable;
  - `upsample_bilinear`.
- **Statistics and display:**
  - `histogram` into a 4096-bin `Histogram`, on a strided subsample, with `percentile` and
    `auto_window`;
  - `Lut` from a `DisplayMapping` (window, gamma, and a grey, inverted or colour colormap), to
    `u8` or RGB.
- **Focus measures:** the variance of the Laplacian, Tenengrad, and the normalized variance, on
  any view.
- **Rayon row bands** by recursive `join`, through the new `ImageViewMut::split_at_row` and
  `ImageViewMut::reborrow` in `siq-types`.
  - Reductions combine in a fixed order.
  - Small per-band state is on the stack, and outputs are the caller's.

## Interfaces

- **API:** `siq_imgproc::{correct, correct_in_place, Calibration, downsample_area, bin, BinMode,
  copy, gaussian_blur, MAX_SIGMA, upsample_bilinear, histogram, Histogram, BINS, Lut,
  DisplayMapping, Colormap, focus, FocusMeasure, Sample, KernelError}`.
- **P2.2:**
  - runs `correct`, `downsample_area`, `histogram`, `auto_window`, `Lut::apply` or `apply_rgb`,
    and `focus`, on the frame-path pool;
  - keeps one set of output buffers per stream;
  - rebuilds the `Lut` when the display settings change.
- **P1.6:** `downsample_area`, `gaussian_blur` and `upsample_bilinear` on `f32` renders.
- **P5.3 and P5.4:** `correct` and `correct_in_place` with their dark frames and gain maps.

## Deviations

1. **No criterion benches.** The `kernels_bench` example measures the budget (conventions §5.3
   allows either). criterion would bring a large dev-dependency tree.
2. **Binning takes any factors,** not only powers of two.
3. **The kernels have only the caller-provided-buffer form,** as the strategy is caller-owned
   buffers (ADR-0025).
4. **`siq-types` gains `ImageViewMut::split_at_row` and `reborrow`.**
5. **Three kernels were made faster after the first measurements:**
   - the area average, from 28 to 19 ms;
   - Tenengrad, from 10.3 to 1.4 ms;
   - 2 × 2 binning, from 24 to 16 ms.

**`kernels_bench`,** release build, development PC, 102 MP frame, 8 threads (half the cores),
best of 5:

| Work | Time | Budget (architecture §4.2) |
|---|---|---|
| Flat field (dark and gain) | 36.4 ms, 22.4 GB/s read | — |
