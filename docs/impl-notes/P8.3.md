# P8.3 — Out-of-core compositing — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P8.3-composite`, from `main` at `32e4e88`
- **Date:** 2026-10-27

## Summary

- **`siq-stitch::composite`** (ADR-0063):
  - `Bounds::of`, the box of the tiles at their corners, to whole pixels;
  - `composite`: blocks of 2 × 2 chunks in parallel, each gathering the tiles that meet it from a
    grid of cells, sampling each pixel bilinearly at the tile's solved corner (or copying, with
    `integer`), correcting by the flat on a 16-pixel grid of gains, weighting by linear ramps from
    the tile's edges (128 pixels), normalising, and writing its chunks; progress and cancellation
    through `Control`;
  - `bytes`, the most a composite holds.
- **`MappedTiles`** maps frames as they are read, keeping at most `capacity` mapped, the least
  recently used let go first; `TileSource::pixels` gives `Pixels`, a borrowed view or a mapping held.
- **`siq-jobs::kinds::CompositeJob`:** a dataset's frames by key, composited at their corners into
  one image, a plane each `(t, c, z)`, with its pixel size and origin from the calibration; then its
  pyramid; the composite 80 % of its progress.
- **`composite_dataset`:** a scan registered, solved, flat-estimated from its frames, composited and
  its pyramid built, and set against the ideal mosaic.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_stitch::{Bounds, CompositeOptions, Placed, Plane, Pixels, composite::{composite,
  bytes}}`, `MappedTiles::with_capacity`, `StitchError::Cancelled`; `siq_jobs::kinds::CompositeJob`.
- **Changed:** `TileSource::pixels` returns `Pixels`; callers take `.view()`.
- **For P8.4:** the stitch job runs the pairs and the solve, then a `CompositeJob`'s run with each
  frame's corner from the reference plane's solution by `p`; its QA and history follow.

## Deviations

1. **The job is the composite and the pyramid,** in `siq-jobs` (processing can't use the job
   system); the stitch job (P8.4) runs the registration and the solve before it.
2. **The PSNR is against an ideal without the tiles' optics or noise,** so its threshold is 25 dB,
   from the measured 28.0; what it tells is the true corners' 6 dB and more over the stage's.
3. **The seam metric** is two neighbours composited apart, differenced over their overlap in
   blocks of 16 × 16: a seam is a step in brightness, which the blocks keep and the noise doesn't.
   Against the ideal, the blocks kept the optics' blur, and told nothing of seams.
4. **The tests' flat is the simulator's own:** nine frames of grains are too few for the
   estimator's medians (23.8 dB with it, against 26.1 dB with none). The example estimates its
   flat from twelve frames of cells.

## Measurements

- **Against the ideal:** 28.0 dB at the true corners, 16.2 dB at the stage's.
- **Seams:** 0.10 % through the flat; 4.7 % and 3.0 % without it.
- **Memory:** 5.0 MB held for a 3481 × 2610 mosaic (18.2 MB) on two threads, against 13.4 MB
  estimated.
- **`composite_dataset`** (release, 16 threads): 12 tiles of 2048 × 1536 over cells, all 17 pairs
  trusted, registered and solved in 36 ms; the flat in 23 ms; 7304 × 4162 pixels (30.4 MP)
  composited in 0.20 s (149 MP/s), the pyramid's 6 levels in 0.17 s; 35.4 dB against the ideal
  mosaic once the stage's mean error is taken out. Smoke mode: 33.6 dB.
