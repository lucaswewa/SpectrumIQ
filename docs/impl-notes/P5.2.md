# P5.2 — Stage–camera calibration — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `103f3be`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P5.2-calibration`, from `main` at `239920b`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **`siq-calib`** (L3, ADR-0049): the fit of `L` from moves and shifts, by least squares with an
  offset common to the moves; the residuals on the stage, in µm, and the thresholds; the backlash;
  `CalibrationRecord`; and the `CalibrationRegistry`, one JSON file per calibration in the
  settings folder, the newest in force.
- **`siq-workflow`** (L4), its first module, `routines::stage_camera`: a star of 16 places at 0.4
  and 0.3 of the field, each approached from the same side, each frame registered against the
  first; the backlash from four pairs of arrivals from either side.
- **The `calibration` Thing:** `status`, each objective's calibration in force; `stage_camera`,
  the objective in use calibrated, kept, and stamped on its frames from then on.
- **References:** the acquisition's context carries the objective in use and its calibration, set
  at start, by `objective.select` and after a calibration. A snap's dataset keeps a copy of the
  record in its manifest, and takes its pixel size from it.
- **Configuration:** `[server] settings`, and `[sim.mount]`, how the simulated camera sits: the
  truth to recover.
- **`calibrate_sim`:** the simulated instrument calibrated, found against injected.

- **For P5.3:** shading calibrations go in the same settings folder, per objective × channel, and

- **For P5.4:** the wizard selects the objective, then runs `stage_camera`; `status` tells which

- **For M7 and M8:** `registry.get(meta.calibration)` gives the frame's `L`, as
  `StageToPixel::for_frame` takes it (`record.affine()`).

## Deviations

1. **`stage_camera` takes no objective.** It calibrates the objective in use, the only one that
   can be: `objective.select` first.
2. **The thresholds are in µm, not pixels.** In pixels, every calibration at 10× was refused: the
   stage's 0.5 µm of noise is 1.3 px there.
3. **The moves reach 0.4 of the field, not a third.** At a third, the rotation's spread from the
   stage's noise was about 0.03° on a 770 µm field, too near the 0.05° criterion.
4. **The test uses a 4096 × 3072 sensor, binned 2 × 2.** Precision goes with the field: on
   1024 × 768, scale y was 0.45 % off. Binning keeps the frames at 3 MP, and tests the shifts'
   scaling.
5. **A snap's dataset records the calibration,** for REQ-CAL-003: a copy in the manifest, and the
   pixel size from it.
6. **The server's tests use no settings folder of their own:** `config()` points at an empty one
   in the target folder, so that a calibration made by hand isn't stamped on their frames.

**Dependencies:** none new. `siq-calib` uses `chrono`, `schemars`, `serde`, `serde_json`,
`thiserror` and `tracing`, all already in the workspace.

**Where the error comes from,** found on the way: without the stage's noise, the shifts still
strayed from the truth by up to ±0.7 px, in a pattern that repeated every 20 µm. The GRBL emulator
moves in whole steps of 0.625 µm, as the controller does. Between steps, a place lands up to
0.31 µm off. Registration agreed with itself to 0.01 px across every option, and the shifts
followed the steps exactly.
