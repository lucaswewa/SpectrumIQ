# P2.4 — Peripherals, channels and system routes — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P2.4-peripherals`, from `main` at `e4b9ecf`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **Four peripheral Things** in `siq-server`, on the simulators of P1.10b. Each is served only if
  its `[peripherals]` section is present (ADR-0034, amended):
  - `illumination`: the levels, and `set_level` by a source's name;
  - `filter_wheel`: the filter, and `select`;
  - `objective`: the objective, and `select`, which also sets the frames' objective;
  - `modality`: the mode and its pattern, and `set_modality`.

  Each mirrors its device's status into observable properties, and emits `changed` on arrival.
- **The `imaging` Thing** (ADR-0035): the configured channels, and `select_channel`. It applies a
  channel in this order:
  1. every light off;
  2. the filter;
  3. the exposure and the display colour;
  4. the channel's light;
  5. half an exposure later, the frames' channel.
- **The system routes:**
  - `/api/siq/system/metrics`: the registry's snapshot, with the acquisition's rate and losses as
    gauges;
  - `/api/siq/system/memory`: the pool, the process's working set, and the budgets.
- **`siq-buffer`:** `process_memory()`, through `K32GetProcessMemoryInfo`.
- **`siq-acquire`:** `Acquisition::with_pool` and `pool_stats()`. The service now makes the pool
  itself, with its metrics in the service's registry.
- **`examples/python/channels.py`** cycles through the channels and saves a snap of each. It needs
  only the standard library.

## Interfaces

- **API:**
  - `siq_server::{imaging, peripherals}` hold the new Things;
  - `siq_server::devices::Peripherals` holds the fitted devices, and so does
    `Instrument::peripherals`;
  - `siq_buffer::{process_memory, ProcessMemory}`;
  - `siq_acquire::Acquisition::{with_pool, pool_stats}`.
- **HTTP:**
  - the Things' affordances are in ADR-0035, and in the snapshots `server__things.snap` and
    `server__openapi_paths.snap`;
  - the metric names are in `peripherals__metrics.snap`.
- **For P5.2 and P6.1:** `imaging::apply(instrument, channel)` is the channel switch, and
  `until_frames_begin_after` is the labelling wait. Both are crate-private: the workflow engine
  will need them public, or will call `select_channel`.

## Deviations

1. **Channel switching is tested in real time, not paused time.**
   - Why: the service runs on the system clock, and the simulators wait in short steps of real
     time. Paused tokio time would not drive them.
   - Instead, the test samples the simulator's true state on a thread throughout a switch from
     DAPI to GFP. That checks the order the light path actually went through. It also checks the
     timing: at least the 110 ms that the light, the wheel and half the exposure take.
2. **A channel's filter is a name, not a position.** This is the configuration's model since P0.3b,
   and ADR-0035 keeps it.
3. **The memory route reports the configured budgets, not caches.** There are no caches yet: the
   tile cache comes in P4.5.
4. **`objective.select` also waits half an exposure before labelling frames,** as channels do. The
   brief asked only that the context be updated.

## Follow-ups

- **The simulated camera images transmitted light only.** A fluorescence channel's snap is a
  brighter brightfield, tinted; GFP's 80 ms at 60 % saturates. Emission through the filter would
  need a fluorescent specimen in `siq-synth` and a filter model in `siq-sim`. No phase plans it
  yet, so it is raised here.
- **The simulator starts with every light at full level** (`TrueState::new`). So, before the
  first channel is applied, live view shows every source at once. The service could apply the
  first channel at start-up; that is left to P3.4's UI work.
- **P6.1:** expose `imaging::apply` for the workflow engine.
- **P10.5:** the real peripherals must report arrival truthfully: the channel order depends on
  their `select` and `set_level` waiting for it (ADR-0035).
