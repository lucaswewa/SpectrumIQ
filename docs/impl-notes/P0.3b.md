## Summary

- **`InstrumentConfig`** from TOML:
  - `[server]`, `[memory]`, `[log]` (P0.3a's `LogConfig`);
  - `kind`-tagged `[camera]`, `[stage]` and `[focus]`;
  - `[[objectives]]` and `[[channels]]`;
  - optional `[peripherals.*]`;
  - `[sim]`, with its world and injected errors.
- **Defaults** for everything a working instrument can do without. The memory defaults are the
  32 GB / 8 GB workstation's.
- **Strict parsing:** unknown keys are errors. Colours are a type, so a bad one is located by the
  parser.
- **Validation collects every problem,** sorted by position, as
  `<file>:<line>:<column>: <path>: <message>`.
  - Parse and type errors are located by the TOML parser.
  - Rule violations are located through a span lookup of their path in the parsed document.
- **Relative paths** are resolved against the configuration file's folder.
- **`json_schema()`.**
- **Shipped files:** `config/sim.toml`, every device simulated, and `config/hardware.example.toml`.
  `.gitignore` ignores the `data/`, `scratch/` and `logs/` folders that `sim.toml` points at.

## Interfaces

- **API:** `siq_config::{load, from_toml, json_schema, InstrumentConfig, ConfigError, Problem}`, and
  the section types in `siq_config::model`.
- **P2.3 builds on:** `load`, the device kinds, `json_schema()` for `--print-config-schema`, and
  `[log]` for `siq_telemetry::init`.
- **Device phases extend their kind's section:** P1.7, P1.8, P1.10 and P10.x. Every new key gets a
  default (ADR-0020).

## Deviations

1. **Split from P0.3:** see `docs/impl-notes/P0.3a.md`.
2. **Simpler messages after the split decision.** Messages are written to follow the value's path
   ("`memory.job_ram_gb`: must be positive"), and duplicates read
   "`channels[1].id`: `channels[0].id` has it too". `check` takes its message directly rather than
   as a closure. That saved about 40 lines with no loss: formatting a message costs nothing at
   start-up.
3. **`[sim] seed` defaults to 0.** `sim.toml` sets 1.
4. **Validation doesn't check that files exist,** such as a dialect. It stays pure and testable, and
   the component that opens a file reports its errors.
