# P1.6 — Image formation model (`siq-sim::optics`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P1.6-optics`, from `main` at `652ae8b`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **A new crate, `siq-sim` (L2),** with the `optics` module.
- **`render_signal`:**
  - the world sampled in parallel on the frame's pixel-to-stage mapping;
  - defocus from `z_coarse + focus + surface_z`, with σ from NA, λ and n;
  - one blur when the defocus is uniform, and up to six blended levels when it varies;
  - coarse sampling when strongly blurred;
  - then vignetting × PRNU × binned area.
- **`digitize`:**
  - shot noise, Poisson below 20 e⁻ and Gaussian above;
  - read noise per binned pixel;
  - conversion, dark offset and DSNU;
  - rounding and clipping to the format.
  - The noise is counter-based: a hash of the sensor pixel and the frame, into a table of 64 Ki
    Gaussian quantiles.
- **`form_image`:** both, at full detail.
- **`Renderer`, the noiseless cache:**
  - noise only while the signal holds;
  - a draft of about 6 M samples on a new pose;
  - the full detail on the next frame at that pose;
  - buffers that only grow.
- **`siq-imgproc` gains `for_each_row`,** the row bands for other crates' per-pixel passes.

## Interfaces

- **API:** `siq_sim::optics::{Shot, OpticsModel, SensorModel, Vignetting, render_signal,
  digitize, form_image, Scratch, Rendered, Renderer}`.
- **P1.7:**
  - builds a `Shot` from `TrueState` (pose with injected errors), the calibration and its
    `CameraSettings`;
  - calls `Renderer::render` into a pool slot's `image_mut::<u16>`;
  - gives `seq` as the frame number;
  - decides the timing of the slow first full-detail frame after a stop.
- **Configuration (P1.7):** the optics and sensor parameters join `[camera] kind = "sim"`, with
  these defaults.

## Deviations

1. **`form_image` takes a `Shot` and a frame number,** not separate pose, affine, ROI, binning
   and an `rng`. The noise is counter-based.
2. **Drafts come from a sample budget,** about 6 M, not a fixed resolution. At first a fixed 1/2
   draft gave 1.3–1.6 frames/s at 100 MP, below the target of 3.
3. **Volumes are rendered with their surface at 0.**
4. **The example writes a PNG preview, not a JPEG.**
   - The planned encoder, `jpeg-encoder`, is licensed "(MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND IJG".
   - IJG isn't on the licence allow-list.
   - This is a question for P2.2 (see Open questions).
5. **Three performance fixes from measuring:**
   - buffers that only grow, which saved about 150 ms per switch between draft and full;
   - drafts from a sample budget;
   - the vignetting's constants worked out once per frame, not per pixel, which saved about
     130 ms at 100 MP.

## Measurements

**Counted lines:** **545**, below 1000 and the brief's ~800.

**`render_frame`,** release build, development PC (16 threads), 11 648 × 8742 at 0.376 µm per
pixel:

| Frame | Time | Rate | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Still pose, noise only | 96–104 ms | 9.6–10.4 frames/s | 9 frames/s ✓ |
| New pose, draft at 1/5 | 266 ms (339 ms the first time, while the buffers grow) | 3.8 frames/s | ≥ 3 frames/s ✓ |
| Full detail, once per stop | 1.57 s | — | — |
| 12 µm defocus, full (sampled at 1/2) | 0.59 s | — | — |
| `fast` profile (25 MP), noise only | 21 ms | 48 frames/s | ≥ 9 frames/s ✓ |
| `fast` profile, draft at 1/3 | 102 ms | 9.8 frames/s | — |

**Where a 100 MP draft's time went** (signal at 1/5 detail, measured on the way):

| Stage | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Signal | about 300 ms | 155–170 ms |
| `digitize` | 82–92 ms | 82–92 ms |

The vignetting's per-pixel `powf` was most of the difference.

The previews were looked at: cells, vignetting, and the defocused frame softer.

**New dependencies:** none. `png` is a dev-dependency here, and was already in the workspace.
