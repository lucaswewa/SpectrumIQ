# P2.2 — Preview pipelines (`siq-preview`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P2.2-previews`, from `main` at `f4a5609`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **`siq-preview`** (new, L4): `Previews` runs three latest-only consumers of an `Acquisition`.
  Each has its own thread and rate cap, and they share a frame-path rayon pool:
  - **overview:** the whole frame, area-averaged to fit 1920 × 1080, aspect kept;
  - **ROI:** a region in sensor pixels, mapped through the frame's region and binning. It is
    clamped, cut to the largest size, and aligned to its zoom of 1:1 or 2:1;
  - **statistics:** the ROI's 256-bin histogram, its 0.1–99.9 % window, and its Tenengrad focus
    measure.
- **Display mapping on the server** (`DisplaySettings` in a watch):
  - a manual window and gamma, or auto-contrast from a strided histogram of the whole frame;
  - grey, inverted or colour maps;
  - the LUT is rebuilt only when the mapping changes.
- **JPEG** with `jpeg-encoder` and its `simd` feature, grey or RGB. The JPEGs go to a
  `PreviewSink`, and the statistics to a `StatsSink`.
- **Metrics:** `preview.<stream>.latency_ms` from mid-exposure, and `preview.<stream>.work_ms`
  for the pipeline's own time.
- **The IJG attribution** is in the README (ADR-0028), and `jpeg-encoder` is in the SOUP register.

## Interfaces

- **API:**
  - `siq_preview::{Previews, PreviewConfig, PreviewSink, StatsSink, StreamKind, PreviewError}`;
  - `siq_preview::{DisplaySettings, Contrast, RoiSettings, Zoom, FrameStats}`;
  - the steps `Renderer`, `fit`, `roi_region` and `measure`.
- **For P2.3:**
  - a `PreviewSink` adapter calls `MjpegStream::add_frame(jpeg)` on the camera Thing's `overview`
    or `roi` stream, by `StreamKind`;
  - the display Thing maps onto `set_display` and `display`;
  - the camera Thing's ROI properties map onto `set_roi` and `roi`;
  - a `StatsSink` feeds a histogram property or event.
- **For P2.4:** the latency histograms appear in `/api/siq/system/metrics` through the registry.

## Deviations

1. **A second latency histogram, `work_ms`.** The brief's measurement, from the frame's
   `host_ts` (its mid-exposure), includes the camera's readout. The budgets of architecture §4.2
   are for the pipeline itself.
2. **Auto-contrast comes from the whole frame,** for both streams. So the overview and the ROI map
   a frame alike, rather than each brightening its own region.
3. **The statistics' window is fixed at 0.1–99.9 %,** whatever the display's contrast. It is what
   the histogram shows.
