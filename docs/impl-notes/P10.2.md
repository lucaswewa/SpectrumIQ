# P10.2 — Camera bring-up and performance tuning — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review: a driver phase is Done once the owner has run its "How to try it"
  ([`siq-euresys`'s README](../../crates/siq-euresys/README.md#how-to-try-it), the
  [runbook](../hardware/camera-bringup.md)).
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P10.2-camera-bringup`, from `main` at `f2e5fff`
- **Date:** 2026-10-29

## Summary

- **Measured,** with the new `hw_acquire_bench` (ADR-0070, amended):
  - the readout is 2.0 ms and 8.63 µs a row, whatever the pixel format;
  - binning reads every row on the Adimec;
  - every mode is within 1 % of that model, none lost;
  - the link at `CXP12_X2` carries the full frame at 12 bits;
  - the grabber's timestamp marks the start of each readout.
- **The frame-rate model:** `FrameRateModel::readout()` and `binned_rows`; the Adimec's map has the
  measured overhead and line time, and `binned_rows = false`.
- **Each exposure's middle,** from the grabber's stamp less half the exposure, on the host's clock
  by the least offset of the last 64 frames: consecutive frames follow their stamps within
  0.02–0.07 ms, where the arrivals jitter by up to 0.6 ms.
- **The frame period** is the readout, or the exposure and 4 ms if that is longer: the Adimec
  skipped every other frame at a long exposure.
- **The camera starts** as the simulated one does: full frame, deepest format, 10 ms, free-running.
- **The acquisition thread** is time-critical.
- **At start,** the service checks the locked pool and the budgets against the RAM
  (`siq_buffer::physical_memory`).
- **`cargo xtask soak --euresys`** runs the soak on the camera, the stage simulated: the operator
  snaps into datasets.
- **The runbook:** [`docs/hardware/camera-bringup.md`](../hardware/camera-bringup.md).

## Interfaces

- **`siq-devices`:** `FrameRateModel::readout(&settings)`, and `binned_rows`, true by default.
- **`siq-euresys`:** `FeatureMap::readout()` and `binned_rows`; the camera's start settings.
- **`siq-buffer`:** `physical_memory()`.
- **xtask:** `soak::Options::euresys`, `cargo xtask soak --euresys`.
- **For P10.6 and P10.7:** the camera's frames are timed and lossless at every mode; the colour
  phases can rely on them.

## Deviations

1. **Two problems on the camera, found by measuring, and fixed:**
   - at a long exposure the camera ran at half its rate;
   - it started in whatever state it had been left: an 8-bit format, which datasets refuse, and a
     350 ms exposure.
2. **No affinity** for the acquisition thread: it is time-critical, and nothing measured asked for
   it to be pinned.
3. **The 10-minute test under full consumer load** ran without the live mosaic, and the recorder
   kept busy by snaps, not scans: with the stage simulated, the stage–camera calibration can't be
   made against the camera, and both need it. They wait for the real stage (M10's amendment).
4. **The camera feature map** was written in P10.1, as the hardware tests needed it; P10.2
   measured its readout.

## Measurements

On the Adimec D-103A12c, with `hw_acquire_bench` at a 1 ms exposure:

| Mode | Frames/s | The model | MB/s by DMA | Lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full, 12, 10 and 8 bits | 12.28 | 12.29 | 2546, 2546, 1273 | 0 |
| 4096 × 3072 | 35.07 | 35.07 | 883 | 0 |
| 2048 × 2048 | 50.82 | 50.83 | 426 | 0 |
| 1024 × 1024 | 91.40 | 92.28 | 192 | 0 |
| Full, binned 2 × 2 or 4 × 4 | 12.28 | 12.29 | 637, 159 | 0 |
| Full, 150 ms | 6.49 | 6.67 | 1345 | 0 |

- **The timestamps:** each region's least lag after its stamp was 52, 61 and 70 ms less than the
  full frame's for 3072, 2048 and 1024 rows: the rows it lacks, at 8.63 µs. The arrivals jittered
  0.15–0.20 ms rms, 0.6 ms at worst, and the clocks drifted by up to 60 ppm.
- **The soak on the camera,** 8 viewers for 10 minutes:
  - 4,725 frames published and none lost;
  - 76 snaps;
  - every viewer received all 3,900 previews of each stream;
  - 147 ms from mid-exposure to the overview's JPEG on average, p95 ≤ 200 ms;
  - the pipeline's work 20 ms, p95 ≤ 35 ms;
  - CPU 8 %;
  - private bytes 2.66 → 2.72 GB.
- **The camera's settings** were as first found after every run.

## Follow-ups

- **The real stage (P10.3's hardware):** the live mosaic and scans on the camera, and the
  10-minute test with all of them.
- **Four links:** cabling the camera's other two links would leave headroom over the 1.9 GB/s the
  full frame needs.
- **The frame-rate model** doesn't add the 4 ms a long exposure's period needs: 6.67 frames/s at
  150 ms where the camera runs 6.49.
