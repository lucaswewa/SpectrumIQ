# P1.10a — ASCII dialects and the dialect emulator (`siq-ascii`, `siq-sim::dialect`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `31f19f8`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P1.10a-ascii-dialect`, from `main` at `08b167b`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **`siq-ascii`** (new, L2): `Dialect`, a device's ASCII protocol read from a TOML file. It gives:
  - the terminators and the step size;
  - the timeouts;
  - the motion: travel, default speed, how the end of a move shows, the polling rate and the
    overshoot;
  - the replies: `ok`, the errors and the completion reply;
  - the commands: `init`, `identify`, `move`, `status`, `stop`, `home` and `jog`.
- **Templates for both directions:**
  - `MA {target:steps}` renders a line from values;
  - it matches a line back through the anchored regex it compiles, with a named group per field;
  - the kinds are `steps`, `um.N`, `mm.N`, `int`, `flag` and `text`, converting lengths at the µm
    boundary.
- **Checks:**
  - syntax, unknown keys and bad templates are TOML errors with their line;
  - every other rule is checked over the whole file, and reported as `path: message`.
- **`siq_sim::dialect::DialectEmulator`:** the device end of a link, from the same dialect. It
  gives:
  - commands answered with their replies, and other lines with the dialect's errors;
  - moves at their speed, jogs to the edge of the travel, and homing;
  - the completion reply for `done = "reply"`;
  - the true focus written to `TrueState`.
- **`config/dialects/example-focus.toml`,** the example dialect, embedded as `Dialect::EXAMPLE`.

## Interfaces

- **API:**
  - `siq_ascii::{Dialect, DialectError, Terminators, Timeouts, Motion, Done, Replies, Commands,
    Command}`;
  - `siq_ascii::{Template, Kind, Value, Values, lengths}`;
  - `siq_sim::dialect::DialectEmulator`.
- **For P1.10b:** `AsciiFocusDrive` uses the following:
  - `Dialect::framing()` for its `LineTransport`;
  - each command's `send.render` and `reply.matches`;
  - `Dialect::error()`;
  - `Dialect::reports("moving" | "homed")`;
  - `motion.done`, `replies.complete`, `timeouts` and `motion.poll_hz`.
- **For P2.3:**
  - `[focus] kind = "sim"`: `Dialect::load(dialect)`, or `Dialect::example()` without one;
  - `DialectEmulator::new(dialect, state, clock).run(device)` on a `siq_serial::duplex`;
  - `kind = "ascii"`: the same dialect, over `siq_serial::open(port, …, dialect.framing())`.

## Deviations

1. **P1.10 was split into P1.10a (this phase) and P1.10b.**
   - *Why:* the complete phase was built first, and measured about 1330 counted lines against a
     budget below 1000 and an estimate of 850.
   - *Why this split:* as with P1.8 and P1.9, it divides into the device side (the dialect and its
     emulator, about 690 lines) and the host side (the focus driver and the peripheral simulators,
     about 640). Both fit without dropping anything.
   - *Plan changes:*
     - the M1 brief and the roadmap list both phases;
     - P2.3, P10.3 and P10.5 now depend on P1.10b, which depends on P1.10a;
     - the count is 88 phases.
2. **Replies and errors are templates, not regexes** (ADR-0030). A template compiles to a regex
   with named groups, as the brief asked, and can also be rendered for the emulator. A raw-regex
   form is left until a real device needs it.
3. **The brief's `move_abs = "MA {pos:steps}\r"` is `move = { send = "MA {target:steps}" }`.**
   The terminator is the dialect's, and is added to every line.
