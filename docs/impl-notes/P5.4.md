# P5.4 — Calibration UI — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P5.4-calibration-ui`, from `main` at `b135b2d`; the import commit
  `cba963a` copies `microscope`'s wizard unchanged
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **The calibration wizard, from `microscope`** (`web/src/calibration/`, ADR-0051):
  - the dialog, Back and Next, Skip until a step has done its part, and closing asked first;
  - tasks per objective (stage–camera) and per objective and channel (dark and flat), built from
    the `calibration` Thing's `status`;
  - three steps: a guide, with the live image and the d-pad; putting the objective and channel in
    use; and running a calibration, with what it found and Again.
- **The first-run prompt:** the wizard opens by itself, after a welcome, once in each page's life
  for each objective that comes into use lacking a calibration.
- **The Calibration page** (`/#/calibration`):
  - each objective with its turret position, parfocal offset, stage–camera calibration and scan
    gate;
  - each objective and channel's dark and flat;
  - a task for any of them, and everything missing.
- **The gating rules** (`calibration/gating.ts`): a scan blocked without its objective's
  stage–camera calibration, and warned of a missing flat or a calibration over 90 days old.
- **The calibration store**, mirroring the Thing; and **`/#/dev/calibration`**, the page and the
  wizard against a fake instrument.

## Interfaces

- **Web:**
  - `useCalibrationStore` (`status`, `stageCamera`, `captureDark`, `captureFlat`);
  - `useCalibrationWizard` (`open`, `tasks`, `inUse`, `start(tasks, welcome?)`, `openNeeded`);
  - `stageCameraTask`, `shadingTask`, `neededFor`, `allNeeded`, `channelsOf`, `summary`;
  - `scanGate(status, objective, channels, now?)` and `STALE_DAYS`;
  - `useJog({ keys })`.
- **For M6:** the Scan page holds its Start to `scanGate(...).ready`, and shows its blockers and
  warnings. The workflow engine applies the blocking rule on the server.
- **Server:** none new. The page reads `calibration/status` and `objective/objectives`, and runs
  `stage_camera`, `capture_dark`, `capture_flat`, `objective/select` and
  `imaging/select_channel`.

## Deviations

1. **Accept or redo, without an unkept calibration:** every run that passes the server's checks is
   kept, and the newest is in force. Accepting is going on, and redoing runs it again (ADR-0051).
2. **Tasks come from `status`, not from each Thing's `calibration_required`,** as in `microscope`:
   SpectrumIQ calibrates per objective and channel.
3. **The first-run prompt is per objective, as it comes into use,** not one wizard for everything.
   *Calibrate what's missing* on the Calibration page runs everything at once.
4. **Objective setup is shown, not edited:** the turret positions and parfocal offsets come from
   the configuration, which an administrator edits (M9 has the admin page).
5. **The wizard's d-pad leaves the arrow keys to the page beneath** (`useJog({ keys: false })`):
   two registrations of the same keys throw.
