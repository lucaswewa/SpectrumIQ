## Summary

- **`siq-stitch::solve`** (ADR-0062):
  - weighted least squares over each tile's deviation from its stage position: the trusted edges
    at a quarter pixel, each tile held to the stage's 5 µm;
  - the normal equations, shared by x and y, factored by Cholesky below 500 tiles, or solved by
    conjugate gradients preconditioned by their diagonal;
  - Huber's weights from the edges' robust spread, reweighted up to 10 times; then the edge that
    disagrees most rejected while out of bounds (4 standard deviations and a pixel), and the rest
    solved again;
  - each component of the used edges anchored at its stage mean, a tile without an edge at its
    own;
  - the report: each edge's residual, weight and use; each tile's move, worst residual and
    component; the components, method, RMS and largest residual, and solves.
- **`siq-stitch::pairs`:** an edge is trusted at a correlation of 0.8, a margin of 0.3 and an energy
  of 50 (ADR-0061, amended), with `PairOptions::min_margin`.
- **`solve_on_dataset`:** a `siq-datagen` scan of 6 × 5 tiles of 2048 × 1536, or a dataset of
  yours, registered and solved, and set against the truth.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_stitch::{Solution, SolveOptions, solve::{solve, Method, Residual,
  TileReport}}`; `PairOptions::min_margin`.
- **For P8.3:** the compositor takes `Solution::positions`, each tile's corner on the mosaic, for
  every channel and plane of the tile's position `p`.
- **For P8.4:** the QA report is `Solution::edges` and `Solution::tiles`; the history records the
  options, `rms_px`, `max_px` and the rejected edges.

## Deviations

1. **Dense below 500 tiles,** not about 2000: a factoring takes 0.78 s at 1960 tiles, against 2 ms
   by conjugate gradients, and a solve reweights up to 10 times a round.
2. **One edge rejected a round:** rejecting every edge over the bound at once took two good edges
   with the two injected outliers on a 4 × 4 scan.
3. **The trust rule changed** in `pairs` (ADR-0061, amended): two right sides of the test's scan had
   energies of 56 and 220, under the 300 that P8.1 set, but correlations of 0.985.
4. **No separate NCC rejection in the solve:** an edge low in correlation isn't trusted, so is never
   used.
