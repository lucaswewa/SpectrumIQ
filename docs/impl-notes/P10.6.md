# P10.6 — Colour I: a Bayer sensor in the simulator, and colour for display — implementation notes

- **Status:** Review, merged to `main` as `1f87fbd`: its colours on the instrument wait for the
  owner's run of its "How to try it"
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P10.6-colour-display`, from `main` at `dc9eb95`
- **Date:** 2026-10-29

## Summary

The camera's raw Bayer frames are shown in colour, live and in the viewer (ADR-0071). The frames
themselves stay raw.

- **The simulator's colour sensor:**
  - `[camera] cfa` for `kind = "sim"`, mono if not given;
  - each pixel takes its colour's share of a warm lamp: red 0.75, green 1, blue 0.55;
  - the filter array is in the camera's capabilities and in each frame's metadata, shifted by the
    region's odd offsets;
  - binned, a pixel keeps its place in the pattern, as the Adimec's does.
- **`siq-imgproc`'s colour kernels:**
  - `demosaic`: bilinear at the mosaic's size, and from whole cells when shrunk;
  - `histogram_cfa`, `white_gains`, `balanced` and `map_colours`.
- **The previews:**
  - the overview from whole cells, and the ROI interpolated at 1:1 and from whole cells at 2:1;
  - the window is green's, and red's and blue's are over their white-balance gains, so there is a
    table per colour;
  - white balance is automatic, from each frame's brightest, or fixed;
  - the flat-field correction applies to each colour;
  - the statistics are per colour, and the focus measure is on luminance.
- **The server:**
  - the `display` Thing's `histogram` gives `colours`, and its `white_from_roi` fixes the white
    balance at the ROI's brightest;
  - a snap's image keeps its frames' filter array, and its channels' windows are green's.
- **The web app:**
  - the histogram draws red, green and blue as a line each;
  - a colour frame gets an "Automatic white balance" toggle and a "White from ROI" button, in
    place of the colormap;
  - moving the window keeps the white balance.
- **The pyramid and the tiles:**
  - `ImageInfo::cfa` is kept in `zarr.json` under `spectrumiq`;
  - the levels keep the cells whole;
  - a colour image's tiles and thumbnail are demosaiced and balanced by its own white.

## Interfaces

- **`siq-types`:** `Cfa::colour(x, y)`.
- **`siq-imgproc`:** `demosaic`, `histogram_cfa`, `white_gains`, `balanced`, `map_colours`.
- **`siq-preview`:**
  - `WhiteBalance`, and `DisplaySettings::white_balance`, `auto` by default and when not given;
  - `Mapping::{Mono, Colour}` from `Renderer::mapping(frame, bits, cfa, settings)`, and
    `Renderer::show` taking it;
  - `FrameStats::colours` and `Counts`, and `Measuring` for `measure`.
- **`siq-storage`:** `ImageInfo::cfa`. A colour image needs even chunks.
- **`siq-pyramid`:** `mean_2x2(view, cells)`.
- **`siq-sim`:** `SensorModel::cfa` and `response`. **`siq-config`:** `SimCameraConfig::cfa`.
- **`siq-server`:**
  - `display.white_from_roi`;
  - the `histogram`'s `colours`, which are `ColourCounts`;
  - `display.lut`'s `white_balance`.
- **For P10.7:**
  - a colour frame's luminance is the 2 × 2 mean of any aligned block, which holds one of each
    colour, as `measure` takes it;
  - the live mosaic still maps a colour frame as mono, through green's mapping
    (`siq-server/src/mosaic.rs`);
  - registration, autofocus, calibration, the shading and the stitch take the mosaic as it is.

## Deviations

1. **The brief's REQ-VIS-005 is traced as REQ-VIS-001.** REQ-VIS-005 is the one Vue codebase.
   The viewer's tiles, which this phase changes, are REQ-VIS-001's: deep zoom with per-channel
   LUTs. REQ-PRV-002 and REQ-PRV-003 also cite this phase, for the ROI and the mapping.
2. **The viewer's white balance is the image's own,** from its top level, not what the operator
   saw live. It suits brightfield; a fluorescent colour's would want it kept with the dataset.
   Recorded as a follow-up.
3. **The simulator's world stays grey.** Its colour is the lamp's, which automatic white balance
   takes away. A world with colours would need a signal per colour, three times the simulator's
   work.
4. **`config/sim.toml` stays mono,** with `cfa` commented in. Until P10.7, registration,
   autofocus and stitching on a colour frame would see the mosaic. P10.7 can switch it on once
   they take luminance.
5. **Two stale limitations removed** from `siq-server`'s README: the Euresys camera "refused
   until P10.1", and "no authentication yet".

## How to try it

1. `cargo run --release -p siq-preview --example preview_to_files -- <folder> gbrg`: the colour
   sensor's overview and 2:1 ROI, neutral, with no checkerboard.
2. The service in colour: copy `config/sim.toml`, uncomment `cfa = "gbrg"`, and run
   `cargo run --release -p spectrumiq-server -- -c <that file>`. Start live view:
   - the histogram shows three lines, blue below red below green;
   - turn off "Automatic white balance", or press "White from ROI", with the ROI anywhere: the
     view stays neutral;
   - `PUT /api/v1/display/lut` with `"white_balance": {"kind": "fixed", "red": 1, "blue": 1}`
     shows the lamp's warm cast.
3. Snap into a dataset, and open it in the viewer: its tiles and thumbnail are in colour at every
   zoom.
4. On the instrument, with the `euresys` service of P10.2's "How to try it": the Adimec's frames
   in colour, live and in a snapped dataset. Set the white with "White from ROI" on a blank of the
   slide.
