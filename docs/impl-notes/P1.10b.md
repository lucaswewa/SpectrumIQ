# P1.10b — ASCII focus driver and peripheral simulators (`siq-ascii`, `siq-sim::peripherals`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `ae0616e`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P1.10b-focus-driver`, from `main` at `31f19f8`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **`siq_ascii::AsciiFocusDrive<T: LineTransport>`:** a `siq-devices` `FocusDrive` over a dialect
  (ADR-0030, amended):
  - one command at a time, each waiting for its reply, with errors, completion replies and other
    lines told apart;
  - `init` and `identify` on connecting;
  - the status polled at `motion.poll_hz`, timestamped at its arrival less half the round trip;
  - a move that ends when the `{moving}` flag clears, or when `complete` comes, within a deadline
    on the clock;
  - a unidirectional approach that arrives going up;
  - `stop`, which fails the move it interrupts with `Stopped`;
  - `jog`, and `home`.
- **`siq_sim::peripherals`** (ADR-0012, amended): `SimIllumination`,
  `SimChanger::{filter_wheel, objective_turret}` and `SimModalityUnit`, behind their traits. Each
  change takes a realistic time on the clock, then sets the true state.
- **`sim_rig`,** M1's exit criterion: the rig of `config/sim.toml` with the real drivers against
  the emulators. Its frames follow the true stage, and blur as the focus leaves the specimen.
- **M1 is complete** with this phase.

## Interfaces

- **API:**
  - `siq_ascii::AsciiFocusDrive::connect(transport, dialect, clock)`;
  - `siq_sim::peripherals::{SimIllumination, SimChanger, SimModalityUnit}`.
- **For P2.3:**
  - for `[focus] kind = "sim"`: `AsciiFocusDrive::connect` over a `siq_serial::duplex`, with the
    `DialectEmulator` serving the device end;
  - for `kind = "ascii"`: the same over `siq_serial::open(port, …, dialect.framing())`;
  - for each `[peripherals.*] kind = "sim"`, the simulator, built as `sim_rig` builds it.
- **For P2.4 and P3.4:** `SimModalityUnit::new(modes, patterns, …)`. The configuration has no
  pattern set yet, so the caller gives one.
- **For P10.5:** each real peripheral driver replaces its simulator, behind the same trait.

## Deviations

1. **The brief's `SimFilterWheel` and `SimObjectiveTurret` are one type,** `SimChanger`, with two
   constructors. They differ only in timing and in the true-state field they move.
2. **A unidirectional approach for the focus drive,** which the brief didn't ask for. `MoveOpts`
   offers it, and z-stacks and autofocus repeat better with it.
3. **The `sim_rig` example, beyond the brief.** M1's exit criterion asks for it, and no phase
   brief named it.
4. **The driver's round trips were written with the complete P1.10,** before the split. They have
   one more case now: two stops at once, which found and fixed a stop that failed `Stopped` when
   another overtook it.
