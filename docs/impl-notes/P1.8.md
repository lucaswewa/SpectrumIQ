# P1.8 — Serial transport and GRBL emulator (`siq-serial`, `siq-sim::grbl`) — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `4129f50`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P1.8-grbl-emulator`, from `main` at `ad88ba2`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **`siq-serial`** (new, L2):
  - `LineTransport`, with `Send` futures;
  - `LineStream<S>` over any byte stream, with `Framing`;
  - `DuplexTransport` (`tokio::io::duplex`) and `SerialTransport` (`tokio-serial`);
  - debug tracing of every line and realtime byte.
- **`siq-sim::grbl`,** a GRBL 1.1 emulator:
  - a synchronous core, timed by its caller, inside an async loop that ticks it every 2 ms;
  - the protocol subset of the brief, with GRBL's error and alarm codes;
  - a planner whose `G4` and `$H` hold up later lines, so `G4 P0` answers after the motion;
  - trapezoidal motion within each axis's rate and acceleration;
  - soft limits (ALARM:2), the homing lock, homing, feed hold and resume, jog and jog cancel,
    and soft reset (ALARM:3 in motion);
  - the `$` settings with GRBL's numbers;
  - reply latency.
- **The true stage:** the commanded position, with scale error, backlash by the direction of the
  last motion, and a seeded random error per move, written to `TrueState` on every tick.
- **Licences:** MPL-2.0 (`serialport`, unmodified) and IJG (P2.2's `jpeg-encoder`, with
  attribution), both decided by the owner. They are recorded in ADR-0028, `deny.toml` and
  conventions §7.

## Interfaces

- **API:**
  - `siq_serial::{LineTransport, SerialError, Framing, LineStream, DuplexTransport, duplex,
    SerialTransport, PortSettings, open}`;
  - `siq_sim::grbl::{GrblEmulator, GrblSettings, GrblErrors}`.
- **P1.9:** the GRBL driver is generic over `LineTransport`, and is tested against
  `GrblEmulator::run` over a `duplex`. It relies on:
  - `ok` on planning;
  - `G4 P0` answering after the motion;
  - status reports in whole steps;
  - the lock at boot and `$X`;
  - the banner after 0x18;
  - ALARM:3 after a reset in motion.
- **P1.10:** the ASCII driver and the dialect emulator use `LineTransport` with a `\r` framing.
- **P2.3:** spawns `GrblEmulator::new(GrblSettings::default(), GrblErrors::from(&config.sim.errors),
  seed, state, clock).run(device)` for `[stage] kind = "sim"`.

## Deviations

1. **Feed hold and jog cancel stop at once,** rather than decelerating.
2. **No missed steps,** which the brief marked optional.
3. **Blocks start and stop at rest,** with no junction blending.
4. **Two licence decisions were made with the owner:** ADR-0028.
