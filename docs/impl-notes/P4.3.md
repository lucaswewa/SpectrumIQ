# P4.3 — Job system — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `c557b1a`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P4.3-jobs`, from `main` at `f971f74`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **A new crate, `siq-jobs`** (L4 Engines), per ADR-0043:
  - **`Job`:** `kind`, `version`, `params`, `estimate() -> Resources`, and `run(&JobCtx)`.
  - **`JobCtx`:**
    - `dataset`;
    - `pool`, the jobs' rayon pool;
    - `check` (`Err(Cancelled)` once cancelled);
    - `progress`, published every hundredth;
    - `log`, to the log and as the last message;
    - `used`, to compare with the estimate.
  - **`Scheduler`:**
    - **the queue:** by priority, then submission;
    - **admission** on each submit, end and cancel, within the RAM and VRAM budgets, with one
      GPU job at a time;
    - **backfill,** and the starvation guard (60 s);
    - **a job bigger than the budgets** runs alone;
    - **each job runs** on its own below-normal thread, a panic failing only that job;
    - **cancelling** a queued job, or a running one at its next `check`;
    - **a job on a dataset** is recorded in its manifest when it starts and when it ends, before
      it is retired;
    - **an exceeded estimate** is counted (`jobs.estimates_exceeded`);
    - **`watch()`** gives a `JobsSnapshot`; the last 100 finished jobs are kept;
    - **`shutdown`** cancels and waits.
  - **`priority`:** `SetThreadPriority(THREAD_PRIORITY_BELOW_NORMAL)` for the job threads and the
    pool's start handler, and `this_thread_priority`.
- **`siq-server`:**
  - **the `jobs` Thing,** with `queue` and `running`, observed from the scheduler's watch, and
    `cancel(id)` outside the global lock;
  - **`Instrument::jobs`,** made from `[memory] job_ram_gb` and `job_vram_gb`, its metrics in the
    service's registry;
  - **on shutdown,** the jobs are cancelled and waited for, after live view and motion stop.
- **`siq-storage`:** `ProcessingRecord::progress`, which defaults to 0 when reading older
  manifests.
- **The web app:** the regenerated OpenAPI document and types, with the `jobs` Thing.

## Interfaces

- **Public:** `siq_jobs::{Job, JobCtx, JobOutput, JobError, JobId, JobInfo, JobState, Priority,
  Resources, Scheduler, SchedulerConfig, JobsSnapshot, this_thread_priority}`.
- **For P4.4 on:** a job submits with its dataset's folder, writes through `JobCtx::pool`, calls
  `check` between steps, and reports `progress`.
- **For P4.7:** the service submits jobs on its `Instrument::jobs`.

## Deviations

1. **Admission is event-driven,** with no dispatcher thread. Every decision follows a submit, an
   end or a cancel, which are the only times the answer can change.
2. **The starvation guard** blocks jobs behind a starved one, rather than raising its priority
   (ADR-0043).
3. **`Resources::threads`** is for information. Admission counts RAM, VRAM and the GPU: jobs share
   one pool, which spreads their threads.
4. **Records use their own id,** the submission time and the job's number, since job numbers start
   again at each start of the service.
