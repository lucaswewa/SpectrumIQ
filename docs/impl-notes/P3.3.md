# P3.3 — Live view — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `0560765`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P3.3-live-view`, from `main` at `b1826ba`
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Summary

- **Commit A, the import** (`d8ad8f3`, not counted): `microscope@0640f99`'s live module, 9 files
  (listed below).
- **Commit B:**
  - **`LiveImage`** streams through the budget (ADR-0037), not its own observer:
    - it reports its frames' size;
    - it says when it waits for a place;
    - it reads SpectrumIQ's connection store and the camera's `overview` and `roi` streams.
  - **`MiniStream`** shows the ROI stream.
  - **The Live page** (`web/src/live/LivePage.vue`, ADR-0038):
    - the overview fills the view, with the ROI outlined on it;
    - the ROI is beside it or in its corner (remembered), with a 1:1 / 2:1 zoom;
    - a control pane has Camera, Display and Focus sections.
  - **`RoiSelector`:** a rectangle dragged on the overview sets `display.roi_region` in sensor
    pixels. Its outline shows the area the ROI stream shows, cut as `siq-preview` cuts it.
  - **`geometry.ts`:** the box, frame and sensor mapping, and `roiArea`.
  - **`HistogramChart` and `histogram.ts`:**
    - 256 bars, linear or log, over the pixel format's range;
    - two markers, by pointer or keys, that make the mapping manual.
  - **`DisplayControls`:**
    - auto or manual contrast;
    - black, white and gamma;
    - the colours: grey, inverted, or the channel's;
    - "Auto once";
    - writes sent one at a time.
  - **`CameraControls`:**
    - start and stop live;
    - snap, with the flash and the snap shown;
    - the exposure;
    - three readout presets;
    - the rate, the frames, and the frames lost.
  - **`FocusSparkline`:** the focus measure and its last 60 values.
- **The example:** `/#/dev/live` is the Live page against a fake camera drawn in the browser
  (`fakeInstrument.ts`), in an app of its own, so it needs no server.

## Interfaces

- **`LiveImage`:**
  - props: `thing` (default `camera`) and `stream` (`overview` or `roi`);
  - emits: `doubleClick` (a point from the image's centre, as shares) and `frameSize`;
  - status: `paused`, while it waits for a place in the budget.
- **`RoiSelector`:** props `readout`, `image`, `region` and `zoom`; emits `select`, a region in
  sensor pixels.
- **`geometry.ts`:**
  - `frameSize`, `shownArea`;
  - `boxToFrame`, `frameToBox`, `frameToSensor`, `sensorToFrame`, `dragged`;
  - `roiArea`, `ROI_MAX`.
- **`histogram.ts`:**
  - `formatBits`, `fullScale`, `shareToValue`, `valueToShare`;
  - `barHeights`, `shownWindow`, `withEnd`, `colormapColour`.
- **`useLiveImagePreferences`:** gains `roiLayout`.
- **For P3.4:** the overview's `doubleClick` for click-to-move. The Live page's control pane takes
  the stage, focus and peripheral sections.

## Deviations

1. **The pane is on the left,** as on `microscope`'s pages: `ControlPane` draws its border on its
   right.
2. **The ROI preview's largest size is a client constant** (`ROI_MAX`, 1920 × 1080),
   `siq-preview`'s default. The server doesn't publish it.
3. **The readout presets align a region to 8 sensor pixels both ways.** Every readout the
   simulator has accepts that; the camera's alignment isn't published.
4. **The copied "View page" test** became a test of the stored preference, since there's no View
   page.
   - The copied `LiveImage` tests now report the image in view on observing, as a browser does.
   - They also keep the client on SSE, since their TD has no socket form.
