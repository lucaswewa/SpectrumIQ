# P4.5 — Tile service and DZI — implementation notes

- **Status:** Done (merged to `main` as `e94680b`)
- **Branch / commits:** `phase/P4.5-tiles`, from `main` at `c7cdbe4`
- **Date:** 2026-10-25

## Summary

- **`siq_pyramid::tiles`** (ADR-0045):
  - **`DziGeometry`:** OpenSeadragon's levels (⌈log₂⌉ of the longer side), each halving rounding
    up, tiles of 254 with an overlap of 1, the tile bounds of `DziTileSource`, and the descriptor.
  - **`render`:** a tile's region at Zarr level `max − l`, halved from the top level for coarser
    ones, through a `TileLut`, encoded as JPEG (quality 85) or PNG.
    - `u16` goes through `siq-imgproc`'s display tables, `f32` the same formula.
    - The colours are grey, inverted, or black to a colour.
  - **`TileLut`:** window, gamma and colormap, checked. `channel_lut` gives a channel's own, from
    `omero`.
  - **`value`:** the true value of a pixel.
  - **`TileService`:**
    - an LRU of encoded tiles, bounded by bytes;
    - keyed on the image's generation (its metadata's time), the tile, the mapping and the format;
    - ETags hashed from the key;
    - images opened once per generation;
    - hit, miss and eviction counts.
- **`siq-server`'s data routes,** under `/api/siq/data/`:
  - the DZI descriptor of a dataset's image's plane;
  - tiles with the mapping in the query, the channel's own by default;
  - `ETag` and `Cache-Control: private, no-cache`, and 304 on `If-None-Match`;
  - the value probe;
  - 404 and 400 with their reason.

  The instrument gains its `Catalog` of `data_root`, scanned again on a miss, and its
  `TileService` within `tile_cache_mb`. Rendering runs on blocking threads.
- **`siq_jobs::kinds::DziExportJob`:** a plane exported as a static DZI pyramid: the descriptor and
  every level's tiles, rendered in parallel on the jobs' pool, with progress and cancellation.
- **Architecture §6.2** gives the routes as built.

## Deviations

1. **The routes name the image:** `/api/siq/data/{dataset}/{image}/{c}/{z}/{t}.dzi`. Architecture
   §6.2's `…/dzi/{channel}/{plane}.dzi` had no image, and a dataset holds several. §6.2 is
   updated.
2. **The value probe takes a Zarr level,** not a DZI level. The viewer converts with
   `max − level`.
3. **Tiles are revalidated, not cached for a time:** `private, no-cache` with an ETag, so a
   rebuilt pyramid is never shown stale (ADR-0045).
4. **The tile cache isn't in the metrics yet:** `TileService::stats` has its counts, for a later
   phase to publish.
